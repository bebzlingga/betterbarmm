                             EXPLANATORY NOTE

           In view of the creation of the Municipal Economic
Enterprise Development Office (MEEDO) a tourism building was
constructed, specifically at Sabang, Bonbon, opposite the sunken
cemetery area.     It is complete with comfort rooms, swimming
equipment, pasalubong center and other commercial facilities.

        The swimming paraphernalia is for rent, the usage of comfort
rooms is for a fee, the pasalubong center items are for sale, and other
facilities are likewise for rent. The comfort rooms are well-maintained,

functional services are available as well as toilet deodorant to dispel
foul odor in observant of proper sanitation.
      Fees and other requirements are in connection with economic
enterprise of the municipality to augment its limited resources.
Therefore the crafting of the following ordinance by this Body is
indispensable to facilitate economic development of this Local
Government Unit.

                         ORDINANCE NO. 01
                           Series of 2014

“AN ORDINANCE LEVYING FEES, CHARGES, RENTALS, FIXING
   PRICE OF PASALUBONG ITEMS FOR SALE AND OTHER
 FACILITIES LIKEWISE FOR RENT TO ANY PERSON WHO MAY
  AVAIL THE FACILITIES HEREIN ABOVE REFERRED TO AND
             PROVIDING PENALTIES THEREOF.”

     BE IT Ordained by the Sangguniang Bayan, this municipality, in
Special Session assembled.

Section 1. POLICY. It is one of the policies of this local government to
         improve its resources for the benefits of the constituencies in
         the locality.

Section 2. DEFINITION OF TERMS.

           2.1 MEEDO - Municipal Economic Enterprise Development Office
           2.2 Resources - the combined income of the Municipal Government
           2.3 Pasalubong Center - a place where items are displayed for sale
           2.4 Swimming Paraphernalia - any device that could help swimmers
                                 float or sink into a deep water (sea) with the
                                 use of oxygen to see view the scenic beauty
                                 underneath the sea
           2.5 Toilet Deodorant - any substance that could help breathing
                                 comfortable, with sweet smell and fragrance
           2.6 Indispensable - could not be dispensed with, necessary
           2.7 Levy - imposition of charges, fees, fixing of prices of
                                 commodities, including rentals.

Section 3. IMPOSITION OF FEES. There shall be levied or collected
          from among the users for the following services:

           Section 4. TIME OF PAYMENT. The fees prescribed in Section 3 herein
                       provided shall be paid to the authorized personnel assigned in the
                       Coastal Eco-Tourism Center immediately prior to the services being
                       rendered.

           Section 5. ISSUANCE OF OFFICIAL RECEIPTS. The Authorized Personnel in
                       the Coastal Eco-Tourism Center shall issue an official receipt as
                       evidence of payment to the corresponding services rendered.

           Section 6. Enforcement. The operation of the Coastal Eco-Tourism Center shall
                      be implemented by the Local Government Unit, through the Municipal
                      Economic Enterprise Development Office, with the oversight function
                      of the Local Finance Committee and the Sangguniang Bayan
                      Committee on Economic Enterprise.

           Section 7. Penalty Clause. Violation of any provision of this ordinance shall
                      subject the violators to a fine of Two Thousand Five Hundred
                      (P2,500.00) Pesos or an imprisonment of not more than Two (2)
                      weeks and shall be required to pay the rates or fees equivalent to the
                      unpaid services as provided for in this ordinance.
