SECTION 7. TERM OF OFFICE. The mandatory representative shall serve

SECTION 8 COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS - The mandatory
representative shall receive compensation, benefits and other emoluments equal to the

The mandatory representative shall have the same powers, duties and functions,

days after its publication in a newspaper of generaf circulation.
