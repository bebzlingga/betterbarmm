MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 2022- 51

AN ORDINANCE DECLARING THE WATERSHED PROTECTION AREAS WITHIN -
THE MUNICIPALITY OF NUEVA ERA, ILOCOS NORTE

WHEREAS, it shall be the policy of the State to prevent, control, and abate pollution of the country’s
water resources; promote environmental strategies for the protection of water resources, formulate a
national program of water quality management; promote commercial and industrial processes and
products that are environmentally friendly and energy efficient;

WHEREAS, Republic Act No. 9275 or the Philippine Clean Water Act (CWA) of 2004
provides that the State shall pursue a policy of economic growth in a manner consistent with the
procedures in the prevention, control and abatement of pollution of the country’s water resources;

WHEREAS, Section 16 of Republic Act 7160 otherwise known as the Local Government Code provides
that:

“Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted those necessarily implied
therefrom, as well as the powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective
governance and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within their respective
territorial jurisdiction, local government units shall ensure and support among other things, the preservation
and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced
ecology, encourage and support the development of appropriate and self-reliant scientific technological
capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic prosperity and social justice, promote full

employment among their residents, maintain peace and order and preserve the comfort and convenience of
their inhabitants.”

WHEREAS, all watersheds that are sources of water for existing and potential irrigable areas and

recharge areas of major aquifers identified by the Department of Environment and Natural Resources and
Department of Agriculture shall be preserved;

WHEREAS, watersheds are beneficial in the tourism industry, agricultural use and sources for human
consumption;

BE IT ORDAINED by the Sangguniang Bayan of the Municipality of Nueva Era, Ilocos Norte by virtue
of the powers vested in law, in session duly assembled that:

SECTION 1. Title. This ordinance shall be known as “AN ORDINANCE DECLARING

THE WATERSHED PROTECTION AREAS WITHIN THE MUNICIPALITY OF
NUEVA ERA, ILOCOS NORTE”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. It shall be the policy of the Municipality of Nueva Era,
Ilocos Norte to promote environmental strategies for the protection of water resources ;

SECTION 3. Coverage. This ordinance shall apply to all the identified watershed areas within the

SECTION 4. DEFINITION OF TERMS: As used in this ordinance, terms are defined hereunder:

1.

Watershed Protection-protects lives, property and the environment of our community by
reducing the impact of flood, erosion and water pollution.

Environmentally Critical areas—areas declared by law as national parks, watershed reserves,
wildlife preserves and sanctuaries; water bodies characterized within the controlled and or
protected areas declared by appropriate authorities.

Contamination-means the introduction of substances not found in natural composition of
water that make the water less desirable or unfit for intended use.

Discharge — includes, but is not limited to the act of spilling, leaking, pumping, pouring
emitting, emptying, releasing or dumping of any material into a water body.

Dumping-means any unauthorized or illegal disposal into any body of water

Hazardous waste —any waste or combination of wastes of solid, liquid, contained in gaseous or
semi-solid form which causes or contribute to an increase in serious irreversible or to
incapacitation reversible iliness taking into account toxicity of such waste.

Poliutant- refer to any substance, whether solid, liquid, gaseous or radioactive, which directly
or indirectly alters the quality of any segment of the receiving water.

Groundwater-a subsurface water that occurs beneath a water table in soils and rocks, or in
geological formation.

SECTION 6. PROHIBITED ACTS. As used in this Ordinance, the following acts are
hereby prohibited:

a)

A

Discharging, depositing or causing to be deposited material of any kind directly or
indirectly into the water bodies or along the margins of any surface water, where
the same shall be liable to be washed into such surface water, either by tide
action or by storm, floods or otherwise, which could cause water pollution or
impede natural flow in the water Oe po4

b) Discharging, injecting or allowing to seep into the soil or sub-soil any substance in
any form that would pollute groundwater.

c) Directly using booster pumps in the distribution system or tampering with the water
supply in such a way as to alter or impair the water quality.

d) Undertaking activities or development and expansion of projects without permission
of local authorities

e)Construction of any structure, such as residential houses, pig pens, or any kind of
structure within the vicinities of the watershed areas.

SECTION 7. MONITORING OF WATERSHED PROTECTION AREAS. All Punong
Barangays are directed to designate pollution control officers and conduct regular monitoring of
the declared watershed protection areas and furnish the Local Chief Executive a monitoring
report.

SECTION 8. PENAL PROVISION. Any person violating the provisions of this ordinance
shall be penalized by a fine of not less than One Thousand Pesos (1,000.00) but not more than
Two Thousand Five Hundred Pesos (2,500.00) in accordance with the Revised Penal Code as the
case maybe.

SECTION 9. REPEALING CLAUSE. All Ordinances, resolutions and other issuances
inconsistent with the provision of this ordinance are hereby repealed and modified accordingly.

SECTION 10. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If for any reason any provision of this Ordinance

is declared invalid or unconstitutional by appropriate agency or office, the remaining provisions
not affected thereby shall continue in full force and effect

SECTION 11. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE. This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.
