                                                            ARTICLE VI
                                                RIGHTS OF SERVICE USERS


                  SECTION 13. SERVICE USER, The determination that a person has a mental health and neurologic
     condition or disability shall be made in accordance with internationally accepted medical classifications and
     standards.


                  SECTION 14. INFORMED CONSENT TO CARE, TREATMENT OR REHABILITATION . The
     consent of the Service User to be treated or admitted in a mental health facility shall be obtained freely,
     without threat or improper inducement, and with pertinent disclosure to the Service User of adequate and
     understandable information in a form or language that helshe understands.


                  SECTION 15. ACCESS TO EFFECTIVE AND HIGH-QUALITY MENTAL CARE - Any person shall
     have the right to receive mental and neurologic health care appropriate to their needs and shall be entitled
     to care and treatment in accordance with the same standards and accessibility as other sick individuals. An
     improved, effective, and easy access to menlal and neurologic health care shall be made possible and a shift
     from a predominantly hospital-based mental health care to community-based care shall be provided.


                  SECTION 16. SERVICE USER'S TREATMENT . A Service User with mental and neurologic illness
     or disability shall have the right to treatment in the least restrictive environment suited to his/her mental health
     needs.


            SECTION 17. VOLUTITARY ADMISSION - Every Service User admitted voluntarily shall have the
     right to be discharged from the facility upon the recommendation of his or her attending psychiatrist. The
     Service User may be retained for fu(her treatment and care in case of the following observations:


              a. There exists a serious likelihood of danger of harming himself/herself or others;
              b       The severity of the Service Use/s mental illness is likely to lead to a serious deterioration in his
