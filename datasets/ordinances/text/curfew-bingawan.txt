AN ORDINANCE PRESCRIBING REGULATION ON CERTAIN MOVEMENT OF MINORS (17
YEARS OF AGE AND BELOW } DURING THE PRESCRIBED HOURS (10:00 PM TO 4:00
AM) FOR REASONS OF PUBLIC SAFETY AND SECURITY.

Section 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as “AN ORDINANCE PRESCRIBING
REGULATION ON CERTAIN MOVEMENT OF MINORS (17 YEARS OF AGE AND BELOW)
DURING THE PRESCRIBED HOURS (10:00 PM TO 4:00 AM) FOR REASONS OF PUBLIC
SAFETY AND SECURITY”.

Section 2. POLICY. This Ordinance is governed by the powers provided in Section 16
of the Local Government Code of 1991 which states that “Every local government unit shall
exercise the powers expressly granted those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as
powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and
those which are essential to the promotion of general welfare. Within their respective
territorial jurisdictions, local government units shall ensure support, among things, the
preservation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the
people to a balanced ecology, encourage and support the development of the appropriate

Section 7. VIOLATION. Minors found violating this Ordinance shall be brought to the
Barangay Hall for verification and record purposes, and, within a reasonable time, turned
over to the Curfew Center and eventually to the parents or guardians of the minor.

Section 8. ENFORCEMENT. The members of the Bingawan Municipal Police Station,
Punong Barangay, Kagawads of the Sangguniang Barangay and the duly appointed
Barangay Tanods are hereby authorized to apprehend and to hold the safety at the
Barangay Hall or Curfew Center for those violators of this Ordinance.

Section 9. RECORDING. The Secretary of the Barangay shall record and maintain a
permanent list of the names, addresses, dates of birth, times and places of apprehension
and other circumstances of every minor apprehended by virtue of this Ordinance.

Section 10. SUSPENSION. The Municipal Mayor upon recommendation of the
Barangay Council concerned may suspend the implementation of this Ordinance in any
particular barangay or other specific areas for a length of time as may be necessary when
public interest so requires.

Section 11. POLICE. The Chief of Police of the Bingawan Municipal Police Station
shall make available the resources of the Police Force to Punong Barangays in the
enforcement of this Ordinance.

Section 12, INFORMATION DISSEMINATION. Copies of this Ordinance shall be
posted at the barangay halls, chapels, churches, schools, public utility vehicles, bus
terminals and other conspicuous places within the barangay and municipality. Public and
private schools shall assist in the information drive through regular classroom discussion
about this Ordinance.

Section 13. ADMINISTRATIVE CLAUSE. The Municipal Social Welfare and
Development Office shall promulgate rules to implement the provisions of this Ordinance
by creating specific procedures for repeat offenders, and provide recreational, educational,
religious, anti-drug and anti-gang programs.

Section 14, SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If for any reason or reasons any part or
provision of this Ordinance shall be held unconstitutional or invalid, other parts or
provision hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue in full force and effect.

Section 15. REPEALING CLAUSE. Any municipal or barangay Ordinance or parts
thereof inconsistent herewith are hereby repealed, revised or otherwise modified

accordingly.

Section 16. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE. This ordinance shall take effect on the day
following its posting in the bulletin board at the entrance of the Municipal Hall and in at
least two (2) other conspicuous places in the municipality for a minimum period of three
(3) consecutive weeks.

ENACTED, this 28¢ day of March 2018.
