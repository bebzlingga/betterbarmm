MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 27
Series 2020

“AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE OPERATION AND MANAGEMENT
OF PIGGERY, POULTRY AND OTHER LIVESTOCK RAISING IN THE
MUNICIPALITY OF CAMALIGAN"

Sponsor: Councilor Nathaniel F. Capucao

WHEREAS, under Section 447(a) of the Local Government Code of 1991, to wit: “The
Sangguniang Bayan, as the legislative body of the municipality, shall enact
ordinances, approve resolutions and appropriate funds for the general welfare of
the municipality and its inhabitants pursuant to Section 16 of this Code and in the
proper exercise of the corporate powers of the municipality as provided for under
Section 22 of this Code”;

WHEREAS, under Section 447(a)(1)(vi) of the Local Government Code of 1991, to wit:
“The Sangguniang Bayan shall protect the environment and impose appropriate
penalties for acts which endanger the environment, such as dynamite fishing and
other forms of destructive fishing, illegal logging and smuggling of logs,
smuggling of natural resources products and of endangered species of flora and
fauna, slash and burn farming, and such other activities which result in pollution,
acceleration of eutrophication of rivers and lakes, or of ecological imbalance”;

WHEREAS, under Section 447(a)(4)(ii) of the Local Government Code of 1991, to wit:
“The Sangguniang Bayan shall require that buildings and the premises thereof
and any land within the municipality be kept and maintained in a sanitary

Section I. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE

REGULATING THE OPERATION AND MANAGEMENT OF PIGGERY, POULTRY
AND OTHER LIVESTOCK RAISING IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CAMALIGAN."

Section If. PURPOSE. As enshrined in the 1937 Philippine Constitution, it is the
declared policy of the State to protect and promote the right to health of the people
and instill health consciousness among them and to protect and advance the right
of the people to a balanced and healthful ecology in accord with the rhythm and
harmony of nature. In pursuance of these policies, this Ordinance is enacted for the
purpose of regulating all piggery, poultry and livestock raising in the Municipality
of Camaligan.

Section II. OBJECTIVES

1. To ensure that all piggery, poultry and other livestock raising businesses and
activities in the Municipality of Camaligan are compliant with all existing pertinent
laws, rules and regulations, particularly those issued by the Department of
Environment and Natural Resources, the Department of Agriculture and the
Housing and Land Use Regulatory Board, as well as applicable local ordinances
including this Ordinance;

2. To protect public health and safety and promote the general welfare, with particular
focus on minimizing, if not eliminating, foul odor, fly infestation and water
pollution;

3. To assist in the growth of piggery, poultry and livestock businesses and activities
in the Municipality of Camaligan through regulated management and
administration.

Section IV. SCOPE AND COVERAGE

1. This Ordinance shall be enforced within the territorial jurisdiction of the
Municipality of Camaligan;

2. The provisions of this Ordinance shall apply to all applications to establish, put up,
and operate piggery and poultry farms in the Municipality of Camaligan as well as
the expansion of existing poultry and piggery farms. Existing poultry and piggery
farms that are already operating upon the effectivity of this Ordinance shall be
enjoined to comply with the provisions of this Ordinance;

3. In case of existing poultry and piggery farms which ceased to operate for more
than one (1) year, the revival thereof shall be considered as a new application to

Section V. DEFINITIONS OF TERMS. As used in this Ordinance, the following

terms shall be defined as follows:

FATTENING — shall refer to the processes whereby the hogs are fattened up to
200 pounds primarily for slaughter;

FOWL - shall refer to a bird kept for eggs or meat;

HEADS -— include weanings, growers and fatteners; or for poultry, it refers to the
number of fowls;

PIGGERY FARM - shall refer to any parcel of land devoted to the raising or
breeding, depasturing, feeding or watering of pigs/swine, generally under the
management of a tenant or owner;

PIGGERY (BACKYARD) — involving (1) sow and ten (10) heads or less, and
allowed in rural areas only;

POULTRY FARM - shall refer to any parcel of land devoted to the raising of
domesticated fowls which serve as sources of eggs and meat for human
consumption as well as breeding of gamecocks. It includes any species of fowl
such as chickens, ducks, geese, quails, pigeons, turkey, ostrich, guinea fowl and
gamecocks;

POULTRY (BACKYARD) — involving a maximum of 500 fowls and allowed in
rural areas only;

RURAL AREA — area outside the designated urban area;
SOW - a female adult swine or pig;

URBAN AREA(S) — include all barangay(s) or portion(s) of which comprising
the poblacién, central business district (CBD) and other built up areas including
the urbanizable land in and adjacent to said areas and where at least more than 50%
of the population are engaged in non-agricultural activities. CBD shall refer to the
areas designated principally for trade, services and business purposes.

Section VII. REGULATORY REQUIREMENTS

1. Upon the effectivity of this Ordinance, the Municipal Agricultural Officer shall
conduct a comprehensive census of all piggery and poultry farms in the
Municipality of Camaligan. Such census shall list the facility
owner/operator/contract grower (if on lease - contract of lease and the duration of
the lease). location and area of farm, number of fowls, heads, swine and hogs, and
an inventory of the existing farm facilities;

2. All developers intending to establish, put up and operate piggery and poultry farms
in the Municipality of Camaligan as well as existing poultry and piggery farms
which intend to expand its farms shall secure a development permit from the
Municipal Zoning Officer of Camaligan in accordance with the Municipality's
Comprehensive Land Use Plan;

Any deviation from the zoning classification in acting on the said applications may
be allowed by the Municipal Agriculture Office on valid and justifiable causes or
reasons;

3. All plans, designs, specifications and special features of proposed poultry and
piggery farm buildings should be submitted to the Municipal Engineer for review
and approval prior to the issuance of business permit. The applicable provisions of
this Ordinance pertaining to such standards and guidelines herein provided for
shall form part of the requirements in the issuance of a building permit and business
permit;

4. Plans for biogas digester must be presented to the Municipal Task Force for review
and approval before construction;

5. All commercial piggery and poultry farms must be registered with the Municipal
Agriculture Office (MAO) and Municipal Health office (MHO);

6. All animal farms, whether backyard or commercial, are required to register their
farms with the Barangay where they are located;

7. The fees payable on applications for permits and registration and upon the issuance
renewal thereof shall be in the amount imposed by the Municipality of Camaligan
and the concerned Barangay;

Section VIII. STANDARDS AND REQUIREMENTS

i

1. Piggery and poultry farm shall be situated preferably in a high topography
and broad terrain for good ventilation. For proper drainage, the farm should
be located in rolling terrain. Flood prone areas and other environmentally
critical areas like a source of water supply shall be avoided;

2. Piggery and poultry farms shall not be built on soils with high moisture levels.
The soil must be well-drained and porous;

3. Commercial piggery and poultry farms shall be surrounded by a buffer zone to
be planted with trees or shrubs;

4. Piggery and poultry farm shall not be located within a 25 meters radius from
sources of ground and surface drinking water;

5. Commercial piggery and poultry farms shall be at least 500 meters away from
the built-up areas (residential, commercial, institutional and industrial) as
designated in the Municipality's Comprehensive Land Use Plan while
backyard poultry and piggery farms shall be at least 200 meters away from
such built-up areas;

6. Establishment of commercial farming shall be located in accordance with the
zoning classification of the Municipality;

7. Commercial piggery farms shall be 500 meters away from the national
highway and major roads while commercial poultry farms shall be 200 meters
away from the national highway and major roads;

8. Commercial piggery and poultry farms shall be at least one (1) kilometer away
from one another to minimize the spread of pests and to reduce the
concentration of air pollutants in a given area. Such a gap of distance for
backyard poultry and piggery farms shall be 500 meters;

9. Piggery farms and backyard are strictly required to construct biogas digester
or standard septic tank for animal waste disposal and conform with the
following requirements:

a. It shall be watertight;

b. It shall be built on concrete and size proportional to the stock population;

c. It shall not be constructed under any building within a twenty-five (25)
meters radius from the nearest groundwater.

Section IX. REGULATED ACTS

1. Existing poultry and piggery farms shall be obliged to use chemical control devices
for their larvae to minimize the source of pollutants;

2. It shall be unlawful to directly discharge animal wastes in any irrigation canal,
river, or riverbanks or any body of water;

3. All poultry and piggery farms must use such a diet for their animals that minimize
foul odor in their waste and discharge.

Section X. PENAL CLAUSE. A penalty amounting to One Thousand Pesos
(Php1,000.00) shall be imposed to all violators on the 1“ offense, Two Thousand
Pesos on the 2™ offense, and upon the 3" offense, the fine shall be Two Thousand
Five Hundred Pesos (Php2,500.00) and/or closure of the animal farm or
imprisonment of not exceeding six (6) months upon the discretion of the Court.

Section XI. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If for reason or reasons, the provision or
provisions of this Ordinance shall be held invalid or unconstitutional, other parts
thereof which are not affected shall continue to be in full force and effect.

Section XII. REPEALING CLAUSE. All ordinances, rules, and regulations or any part
thereof, whose provision are in conflict to the provisions of this Ordinance are
hereby repealed, amended, and modified accordingly.

Section XIII. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE. This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval
and publication in a newspaper of local circulation in the Province of Camarines
Sur for two (2) consecutive weeks.
