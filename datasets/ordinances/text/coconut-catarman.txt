                            ORDINANCE NO. 12
                              Series of 2015

 “AN ORDINANCE PROTECTING COCONUT PRODUCERS AND FARMERS
  FROM THIEVERY OF COCONUTS, NAMELY: MATURED (TIBUOK LUBI)
              AND YOUNG COCONUTS (BUTONG).”

BE IT ORDAINED by the Sangguniang Bayan, this municipality, on its regular
        session assembled:

Section 1. TITLE. This ordinance shall be known as “Anti-thievery of Coconuts,
          matured (tibuok lubi) and young (butong) coconuts.

Section 2. STRICT COMPLIANCE. All coconut, copra and butong producers
          are hereby required to secure permit from the Office of the Punong
          Barangay from the place where the plantation is located immediately
          prior to harvesting.

Section 3. The Office of the Barangay is hereby authorized to levy an amount of
          Twenty (P20.00) Pesos per parcel of coco plantation for every permit
          issued which shall be accrued to the barangay funds.

Section 4. PENALTY CLAUSE. Violation of this ordinance shall subject the
         violation to a fine of not more than P2,000.00 and/or imprisonment of
         not more than sixty (60) days, or both, at the discretion of the proper
         court.

Section 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If for any reason or reasons, any part of
           this ordinance is held unlawful or invalid, other parts and provisions
           hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force
           and in effect.

Section 6. EFFECTIVITY. This ordinance shall take effect after public hearing
           and posting of this ordinance in conspicuous places within the
           Municipality of Catarman shall have been made.

APPROVED this 12th day of August 2015.
