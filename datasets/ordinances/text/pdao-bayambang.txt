SECTION 3. Rationale. The Persons wittr Disabilities (PWDs), due to various kind of restrictions in
their abilities, are arguably the most disadvantaged sector in the society. Most of them even lacked
the educational attainment necessary for seH-advancement. Thus, the Municipal Govemment of
Bayambang needs to consider the plight of the PWD sector as a priority concem. This ordinance
provides a mechanism by which to uphold, promote and protect the rights and welfare of Persons
with Disabilities in Municipality of Bayambang, adopting measures for them to avail the privileges and
incentives provided by law, and ensudng that they are made more proactive, responsible, and
productive stakeholders of society.
