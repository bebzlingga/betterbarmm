AN ORDINANGE CREATING THE MUNICIPAL YOUTH DEVELOPMENT OFFICE AND
THE MUNICIPAL YOUTH DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF
ARTECHE, EASTERN SAMAR, PROVIDING FUNDS FOR THE PURPOSE THEREOF.

MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 2019-03
Series of 2019

introduced and Proposed by: Athena Erika C. Ocfemia,CPA
Chairman, Comm. On Youth & Sports Devt.
Seconder Unanimously Seconded

WHEREAS, Article 2 Section 13 of the 1987 Philippine Constitution states that, “the
State recognizes the vital role of youth in nation building and shall promote and protect their
physical, moral, spiritual, intellectual, and social well-being, inculcating in the youth
patriotism and nationalism, and encourage their involvement in public and civil affairs’;

WHEREAS, under section 25 of the Republic Act 10742, otherwise known as f
Sangguniang Kabataan Reform Act of 2015, there shall be in every province, city and
municipality a youth development office;

WHEREAS, under Section 27 of the aforestated Republic Act, the local government
unit shall incorporate in its annual budget such amount as maybe necessary for the operation
and effective functioning of local youth development office and in accordance with the COA
accounting and auditing rules and regulations;

WHEREAS, the Department of Interior and Local Government Memorandum No. 2017-
26 states that ‘all City and Municipal Mayors are hereby directed to create their respective
Local Youth Development Office (LYDO) within their areas of jurisdiction”,

WHEREAS, the aforestated DILG Memorandum also stated that the LYDO “may be pu'
under the Office of the Local Chief Executive (LCE), the Office of the Planning and
Development, the Office of the Social Welfare and Development, or in any other office
deemed appropriate by the Local Government Unit (LGU)”,

WHEREAS, the same DILG Memorandum also states that “in the event that the LGU will
exceed the prescribed personal services limitations, the LCE may designate existing
personnel whom he or she deems fit to serve this purpose, until such time that the LGU can
ready create the LYDO”,

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED, by the Sangguniang Bayan of Arteche,
tern Samar, in session assembled that:

ARTICLE I. POLICY AND DEFINITION OF TERMS
Section 1. TITLE

This ordinance shall be known as the “Ordinance Creating the Municipal Youth Development
Office and Municipal Youth Development Council of the Municipality of Arteche, Eastern
Samar, providing Funds for the Purpose Thereof”.

Section 4, CREATION OF THE COUNCIL

a) To eneure wide and multi-cectoral youth participation in local governanos, there shall be
created the Arteche Local Youth Development Council which ehall be responsible for the
formulation of policies and implementation of youth development programs, projects and
activities in coordination of various government and non government organizations.

b) Local Registration of Youth and Youth-Serving Organization- all youth and youth-serving
organizations with a minimum of ten (10) members in good standing ane which are operating
in the Municipality of Arteche shall be registered, All youth and youth- serving organizations
must secure a copy of the registration form provided by the Local Youth Development Office
and shall submit them to the same office.

¢) Local Verification of youth and youth-serving organization- the Local Youth Development
Officer shall verify information provided by these organizations. The officer shall then the list
of registered and verified youth organizations to the National Youth Commission.

da) Renewal, Verification and Continuing Local Registration- registered youth and youth-
serving organizations shall renew their local registration and verification every three (3)
years. The Center shall conduct a continuing registration procedure taking into consideration
the budgetary and manpower requirements and the presence of youth and youth-serving in
the locality.

e) Condition Precedent of the Members of LDYC- local registration and verification of youth
and youth-serving organization is a condition precedent for the participation in the Local
Youth Development Council. However, if a youth or youth-serving organization is denied in
local registration and verification, such organization can apply special registration and

verification to the National Youth Commission as provided by law. Such special registration
and verification entitles the organization to participate in the LYDC elections.

f) Qualifications of Members of the LYDC- a member of the LYDC must be a resident of the
Philippines, he or she must be able to read and write in Filipino, English or the local dialect
and must not have been convicted by final judgement involving moral turpitude.

ARTCILE Ill. LOCAL YOUTH DEVELOPMENT OFFICE

Section 12. CREATION OF THE OFFICE

There is hereby created a Local Youth Development Office which shall be housed at the
Youth Center or within the Municipal Building, in the absence of the youth center.

Section 13. FUNCTIONS OF THE OFFICE

The office shall have the following functions:

a) Help register and verify Youth and Youth-Serving Organizations in accordance
with National Youth Commission's Youth Organizations’ Registration Program Guidelines,
ordinances and other existing laws;

b) Provide technical assistance to the Local Youth Development Council in the
formulation of Local Youth Development Plan;

¢) Facilitate the election of Local Youth Development Council representatives;
d) Serve as a secretariat to the Local Youth development Council;

e) Conduct the mandatory and continuing training of SK Officials and Local Youth
Development Council Members, in accordance with the programs jointly designed
and implemented by the National Youth Commission and the Department of the
Interior and Local Government.

f) Provide technical, logistical and other support in the conduct of the mandatory and
continuing training programs, and to such other programs of the Commission and
DILG;

g) Coordinate with the Commission with regards to the youth programs within theix

jurisdiction; and

h) Perform such other functions as may be prescribed by law, ordinance, or as
required by the Municipal Mayor, DILG and the National Youth Commission.

Section 8. MEETINGS AND CONVENING LYDC

a) Meeting of the Council- the council shall meet at least once in every quarter or as often as
it may deem necessary. The Chairman or his duly designated Convenor shall preside in all
Council meetings.

b) Convening the LYDC- any of the Co-Chairpersona (SK Chairperson or Sectoral
Chairperson) with the concurrence of one another may call a meeting. The SK Pambayang
Pederasyon President, or in her/his absence, the Vice-President shall initially convene the
council and shall be given preference to preside the meeting.

Section 9. TERM OF OFFICE

The representatives from the youth organization or youth-serving organization shall vote
among themselves a Sectoral Chairperson and shall serve a term for one year. The Sectoral
Chairperson can serve to a maximum of 3 terms provided there shall be an annual election.

Section 10. YOUTH CENTER

The council may establish a Youth/Teen Center, which shall be located in the municipality
proper. The youth center shall operate as a multi-purpose center or a one-stop shop facility.

Section 11. THE LOCAL YOUTH DEVELOPMENT PLAN (LYDP)

The Council shall adopt a Three Year Local Youth Development Plan. The LYDP shall be
initially be drafted by the SK Federation and shall be finalized by the LYDC. This shall be
submitted to the Local Chief Executive for approval and the same shall be endorsed to the
local Sanggunian. The said plan shall be the basis for the implementation of local youth
development programs and services in coordination with concerned agencies. This plan shall
give priority to projects, programs and activities that will promote and ensure meaningful
youth participation in nation-building, sustainable youth development and empowerment.
The LYDP shall be anchored in the NYC’s Philippine Youth Development Plan and these shall
include, but not limited to, the following:

a) Scholarship and Exchange Programs (GloablMobility)- local and internatio
scholarships and exchange programs, group and subsidies;

b) Sports Recreation (Active Citizenship)- trainings/conferences, seminars, spo
clinics, competition and other sports activities;

c) Livelihood Developments (Economic Empowerment)- trainings on livelihood skills,
basic business management, formal and non-formal skills, market linkaging, business
development, value orientation, credit facility and job placement sourcing;

d) Traning and Capacity Building Programs (Governance)- leadership, advocacy,
values formation, governance, entrepreneurship, information technology and other
relevant programs;

e) Youth Cooperatives- organization of youth cooperatives, technical assistance and
financial support from appropriate government agencies;

f) Culture and Arts- cultural programs and and sctivities workshop, art classes;

ARTICLE IV. FINAL PROVISIONS

Section 16. APPROPRIATIONS

The Municipal Government of Arteche shall allocate seventy two thousand pesos (72,000) for

Section 17. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE

If any of the provisions of this Ordinance is declared invalid or unconstitutional, the
remaining provisions not affected thereby shall continue in full force and effect.
