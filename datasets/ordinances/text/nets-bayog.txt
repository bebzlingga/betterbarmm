                                      MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 13-216-17
                   AN ORDINANCE STRICTLY PROHIBITING THE USE OF MOSQUITO NETS AND
                   TINY-HOLED FISHING MATERIALS IN THE RIVERS, LAKES AND STREAMS IN
                           THE MUNICIPALITY OF BAYOG, ZAMBOANGA DEL SUR.

                                                                    th                                                                                                                  nd
       BE IT ENACTED, by the 13 Sangguniang Bayan of Bayog, Zamboangadel Sur on its 52
Regular Session held at the Municipal Session Hall on August 14, 2017 at 9:00 a.m.

SECTION I – SCOPE: The use of mosquito nets and tiny-holed fishing materials contributed to the
depletion of certain fresh water species important to man’s survival. Our rivers, lakes and streams no
longer provide enough foods to sustain the needs of the inhabitants once dependent on said water
sources as means of living. Aquatic species useful to man are alarmingly vanishing, hence creating an
Ordinance is necessary.

SECTION II - PROHIBITION: It is strictly prohibited to any person or group of persons who shall use
mosquito nets and tiny-holed fishing materials to catch/gather aquatic species in our rivers, lakes and
streams within the Municipality of Bayog.

SECTION III - PENALTY: Violation of this Ordinance shall subject the offender(s) to the following:
                                             st
                       1 Offense . . . . . . . . . . a fine of P1,000.00
                        nd
                       2 Offense . . . . . . . . . . a fine of P2,000.00
                        rd
                       3 Offense . . . . . . . . . . a fine of P2,500.00, or an imprisonment of 30 days, or
both fine and imprisonment as to the discretion of the
court.

SECTION IV. AUTHORITY OF THE BARANGAY TO ENFORCE - The Barangay, thru its Chairman is
authorize to enforce this Ordinance, and shall be entitled to collect 50%of the fine imposed for every
violation committed.

SECTION V. EFFECTIVITY – ThisOrdinance shall take effect after three (3) consecutive weeks of
Postingin conspicuous places and publication in the local news letter of the MunicipalityofBayog, Province
of Zamboanga del Sur.
