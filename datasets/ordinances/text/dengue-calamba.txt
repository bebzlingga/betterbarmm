PREFACE

WHEREAS, dengue is community based problem and that there is a need to intensify community
awareness and participation to successfully minimize or eradicate the vector and the diseases;

WHEREAS, based on record, dengue cases continue to increase causing an alarming public health
problem; ,

WHEREAS, dengue remains a cause of moriality and morbility in the country. All regions, cities and
municipalities are endemic for dengue and Municipality of Calamba, despite its being a highly progressive
Municipality, is not spared from experiencing such an outbreak or epidemic;

WHEREAS, ait present the only method or conitrolling/preventing dengue virus transmission is to
combat the vector mosquitoes;

WHEREAS, considering that dengue is a vector-borne disease transmitted by an infected mosquito,
produced in any artificial containers that hold clear stagnant water, dengue will spread easily;

WHEREAS, vector control can be effectively and efficiently implemented using environmental
management and chemical methods as well as proper solid waste disposal and improved water storage
practices, including covering containers to prevent access by egg-laying female mosquitoes.

NOW THEREFORE, on motion of Municipal Councilor, Hon. Emmanuel M. Cale, duly seconded by
Municipal Council, Hon. Dennis M. Bunao, be it —

RESOLVED, as it is hereby resolved to enact this Ordinance:
ORDINANCE NO. 05-S.2020
Authored by: Hon. Emmanuel M. Cale

genus Flavivirus), transmitted by aedes mosquitoes, and characterized by headace,
severe joint paint, and a rash — all called breakbone fever, dengue fever.

Stagnant Water — montioniess water, no flowing in a stream or current. Also known as

Public establishments — hotels, motels, dormitories, commercial buildings, lodging and
boarding houses, apartments, hospitals, libraries, museums, offices, malls, ,markets,

institutions

Vector — an organism that cay carry a particular disease- -causing agent (like a virus)
without actually developing disease.

Place — land, building, residence, pier, watercraft, or any means of conveyance.

Public Places — parks, plazas, picnic grounds, camps, roads, cemeteries, and memorial
parks, terminals, rest areas, and other open fields and enclosed areas of public assembly.

Integrated Control — controlling pests through the use of several different methods and
procedures which are used to complement each other. These procedures may include the
use of pesticides, environmental sanitation measures, natural as well as mechanical and
biological methods.

Pesticide — any substance or product, or mixture intended to control, prevent, destroy,
repel or mitigate, directly or indirectly any pests.

Urban Pest Control — pest control activities in all habitable areas but not to include
agricultural pest control activities.

Urban Pest Control Applicator — an individual who uses or supervise the use of pesticides
or any methods or services for urban pest control or implementation of a vermin reduction
and mitigation program.

Fumigation — the act of applying, releasing or dispensing a toxic chemical so that it
reaches the target organism primarily or wholly in gaseous state.

Misting — a fine spray of approved chemicals to control or eradicate the vector of the
disease.

Urban Pest Control Operator — refers to establishment or entities engaged in the
application of pesticides or any other methods or services for urban pest control or
implementation or a vermin reduction and mitigation program.

Section 3. Vector Breeding Grounds Control. — building/structures under construction must not
accumulate stagnant water and solid wastes thus all owners, managers, administrators and/or caretaker of
household, schools, vacant lots, abandoned houses, tire trading/merchandiser business, and all other
building/establishment within the Municipality of Calamba are mandated to get rid of vectors breeding ground,
particularly stagnant water, to avoid mosquito larvae (kiti-kiti), otherwise they are presumed to have violated

this ordinance.

Section 5. Prevention and Control. —

5.1 All other interventions especially the use of chemicals other than for research purposes shall
secure Clearance from the Municipal Health Office (MHO) prior to its conduct.

5.2 Pest Control Operators shall be accredited by the MHO nrior to the render of services within

the jurisdiction of the Municipality of Calamba.

5.3 All new technologies, approaches and processes that may developed in the future which will
help in the prevention and control of dengue shall be adopied form implementation upon
recommendation by the MHO.

Section 6. Information Education Campaign (IEC). — The Municipal Health Office with cooperation of
Municipal Environment Office, Liga ng Barangay and the Barangay concerned shall conduct a massive public

awareness Campaign, which include but not limited to, health education including the organization/coordination
and lectures, Ovi-Larvitrapping manner of destroying breeding sites, and participation on the “Oplan Linis”
Activities, as well as disseminate all information relative to the implementation of this ordinance.

Section 7. Rules and Regulations. — The Municipal Health Office shall in coordination with the
Municipal Environment Office (MENRO) and Liga ng mga Barangay, promulgate rules and necessary event,
control and abate the mosquito vector of dengue, subject to the approval of the Municipal Mayor. Such rule

and regulations shall take effect upon publication.

Section 8. Creation of Anti-Dengue Task Force and Deputation of Enforcer. - An Anti-Dengue
Task Force shall be created and organized which shall formulate comprehensive policies, programs and
guidelines on the prevention and control of dengue, likewise, it shall monitor the implementation of the clean-
up drive, coordinate with agencies or offices concetned relative to the information and awareness campaign
against dengue; and create dengue monitoring and prevention team which shall primarily responsible for the
monitoring of the incidence of dengue in 19 barangays in the Municipality of Calamba and submission of a
monthly report attach data collected on dengue cases in the barangays to the Anti-Dengue Task Force and
Municipal Health Office.

Section 9. Penalties — All owners, managers, administrators of the establishments or households who
fail or refuse to comply with the provision of this ordinance shall be punished in accordance with the following
penaities:

1* Offense - Stern written warning by the Municipal Health Office

2" Offense - A fine of Five Hundred Pesos ( P500.00 ) or Social Services (Pahina)

3 Offense - A fine of One Thousand Pesos (P1,000.00) or Social Services (Pahina)

Where the violator of this Ordinance does not contest his/her apprehension and elect to pay the fine
imposed under the preceding paragraph, the enforcement office shall forthwith issue citation ticket to the
person apprehended without resort of the Court. The citation ticket shall state or contain:

{a.) The names, signature, age and address of the person apprehended:

(b.) The materials facts obtaining the commission or omission of the crime defined and
penalized under this Ordinance;

(c.) The amount of fine which is payable in favor of the Municipal Government of Calamba,
Misamis Occidental, and

(d.) Notice requiring the person, apprehended for extra-judicial settlement of his/her violation
