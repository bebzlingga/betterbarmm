                                     ORDINANCE NO.01, SERIES OF 2024

"AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE SELLING, SERVING AND DRINKING OF LIQUOR IN
PLACES OF ENTERTAINMENT, BUSINESS ESTABLISHMENTS AND PUBLIC PLACES AND
PROVIDING PENALTIES FOR VIOLATION THEREOF."



                                             INTRODUCED BY:
                                     HONORABLE JOSEPH B. AGUE, M.D.

government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied
therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and
effective governance and those which are essential to the promotion of the general
welfare;

        "WHEREAS, this is an important health policy for the health problem in the
municipality of Carrascal and statistics show that one of the main causes of road accidents,
bar fights, and petty and serious quarrels can be attributed to liquor drinking;

       "WHEREAS, there is a need to regulate the selling and serving of liquor and
intoxicating dinks to prevent irresponsible consumption which is known to cause unruly
and aggressive behavior - a perfect recipe for trouble and accident;

      "WHEREAS, Section 447 (Powers, Duties, Functions, and Compensation) of the
same code empowers the Sangguniang Bayan to enact ordinances intended to prevent
and suppress activities inimical to the welfare and morals of the inhabitants like habitual
drunkenness in public places and impose appropriate penalties thereof; and

      "WHEREAS, in order to promote a healthy environment, protect the citizens and
reduce the harm from taking and drinking intoxication materials such as alcohol and
beverages extravagantly and without regulation, hence this Ordinance is passed.

                                             " NOW THEREFORE "

         "BE IT ENACTED BY THE SANGGUNIANG BAYAN, in Regular Session assembled
that:

        SECTION 1. SCOPE. This Ordinance shall regulate the selling, serving and drinking of
liquor in places of entertainment, business establishments, and public buildings and
places, and providing penalties for violation thereof.

       SECTION 2. DEFINITION OF TERMS. As used in this Ordinance, the following terms
shall mean:

         a. PLACES OF ENTERTAINMENTS— shall refer to places which include night or day
            clubs, cabarets or dance halls, bar, cocktail, lounges, videoke bars, beer
            gardens, disco pads, casinos, and other similar establishments.

         b. BUSINESS ESTABLISHMENTS -shall refer to those that sell and/or server liquor,
            to include restaurant, carinderias, hotels, liquor stores, wine dealers, sari-sari
            store, bazaars, groceries, and other similar establishments.

          c. LIQUOR - shall refer to all alcoholic or intoxicating beverages such a beer,
             whisky, brandy, gin, rum, vodka, and other kinds of foreign wines and local
             drinks such as bino, tuba, lambanog and other intoxicating concoctions.

          d. MINORS - shall refer to persons who are below 18 years of age.

          e. PUBLIC PLACES -shall include the following: Plaza, public markets, public parks,
             government offices/establishments, monuments, alleys, streets, sidewalks and
             barangays/national roads and other similar establishments.

         f.    PUBLIC BUILDINGS - pertain to buildings occupied by government offices
               including its immediate premises.

         g. SERVE - shall mean to offer, dispense, or tribute liquor for consumption within
            the business premises.


         SECTION 3. LICENSING AND OPERATION.


         a. No new license for selling liquor shall be issued to any places of entertainment
            or business establishment unless there is attached to the application a
            certification issued by the Municipal Planning and Development Office (MPDO)
               that the business establishment or place of entertainment is located beyond
               the radius of one hundred (100) meters from any school, church, clinic, hospital,
               public building and in accordance with the Zoning Ordinance of the
               Municipality.


          b. All business establishments or places of entertainment that are already in
               existence within the one hundred (100) meter radius prohibited zone at the
               passage of this ordinance may continue to sell liquor subject to restrictions
               herein provided. However, upon withdrawal from business or change of its
               ownership/operator, no new license to sell liquor in the same site shall be
               granted. The same applies when a new school, church, hospital, or public
               building is established in a certain area after the passage of this Ordinance.


         c. All convenience stores, bazaars, groceries including sari - sari stores within the
            prohibited zone may sell liquor if their license so provides. However, they shall
            in no case serve liquor for consumption to customers within the business
            premises.


         d. All business establishments selling or serving liquor shall display in the
            conspicuous places inside the establishment an abstract of this Ordinance.



        SECTION 4. SERVICE OF LIQUOR. All business establishment and Entertainments
selling and serving liquor shall sell liquor until 12:00 midnight only.


       SECTION 5. RESTRICTION AS TO PERSONS. Liquor shall neither be sold nor served
to minors, nor shall minors be allowed to drink liquor.

        SECTION 6. GUARD DUTY REQUIREMENTS. Places of entertainment shall employ
at least one (i) designated security guard or person to render guard duty and to bodily
search customers for deadly weapons, explosives, and prohibited drugs during the hours
of operation.

       SECTION 7. POSTING OF NOTICE. All business establishments and places of
entertainment selling or serving liquor shall post a notice in bold letters of permanent
nature measuring two (2) feet by three (3) feet that:

           "PERSONS BELOW 18 YEARS OLD SHALL NOT BE SOLD/SERVED LIQUOR."

       SECTION 8. BAN ON DRINKING OF LIQUOR IN PUBLIC PLACES AND IN VEHICLES.
Drinking liquor in public places shall strictly prohibited. This prohibition likewise applies to
drinking inside vehicles while said vehicles, while said vehicles, either moving or packed
are within public places. However, the prohibition shall not apply during barangay fiestas,
wakes, and municipal celebrations.

        SECTION 9. PENALTIES. Violation of any provision of this Ordinance shall be subject
to the following fines/penalties.

          A. On the part of the owners/operators of places of amusement and business
             establishments:

                1.    Every act committed in violation of the specific prohibitions under section 3,
                      4, 5, and 6 therein shall be fined the amount of Two Thousand Five Hundred
                      Pesos (Php 2, 500.00) for each offense.

          B. OTHERS.

                1. Person drinking in public places and inside vehicles, either moving or parked
                   that are within public places; and
                2. Minors drinking liquor.
                   For Every Offence - Five Hundred Pesos (P500.00)

                     It shall be parents/guardians of minor violators who shall pay for the
                     fines/penalties imposed herein. Minors, guardians, or offenders who cannot
                     afford to pay the P500.00 penalty shall be required to render instead
                     community service to be determined by the Office of the Municipal Mayor.

        SECTION 10. IMPLEMENTING MECHANISM. The Rural Health Unit and Municipal
Treasurer's Office of the Municipality, in coordination with the Philippine National Police
shall lead the implementation of the ordinance.


       SECTION ii. REPEALING CLAUSE. All ordinances, rules, and regulations or parts
thereof whose provisions are in conflict or inconsistent with this Ordinance are hereby
repealed, amended, and modified accordingly.


          SECTION 12. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If for any reason, any section or provision of
this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid by a competent authority, the other
provisions hereof not so affected shall continue to be in full force and effect.
