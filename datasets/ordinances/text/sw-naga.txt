                               Explanatory Note

     The Local Government Code expressly provides that the
collection, management and disposal of solid waste is a
responsibility of both the City and the Barangays. With this
mandate, a system of partnership becomes necessary to ensure
that solid wastes are efficiently managed and properly
disposed;  and that the people involved are       given due
recognition and reasonably compensated for contributing to a
cleaner and greener Naga City.

       Be it therefore ordained, by the Sangguniang Panlungsod,
in session assembled,          that;

       SECTION 1.     TITLE - This ordinance shall                  also be       known   as

the "Barangay Solid Waste Management Volunteers Ordinance                                 of
Naga City".

       SECTION 2.     PURPOSE AND OBJECTIVE -                This    Ordinance         shall
serve as basis for the City's engagement of volunteers who
will   undertake  activities   relative  to  the   management,
disposal,   and  collection  of   solid wastes   within  their
respective territories.

     In addition, this ordinance aims to effectively devolve
to the barangays, while assisting in its implementation, the
mandates of the Ecological Solid Waste Management Act of 2000
or the R.A. 9003 thereby augmenting our efforts in making
Naga City a cleaner and greener City, compliant with the
standards of solid waste management as prescribed under
pertinent   laws  and   issuances  of  concerned   Government
agencies.

       SECTION   3.    LEGAL    BASIS
                                 Section 458(5)(xiii) of the
Local   Government  Code   provides   that  the   Sangguniang
Panlungsod shall approve ordinances which shall ensure the
effective  and efficient delivery of basic      services and
facilities; and provide for an efficient and effective system
of solid waste and garbage collection and disposal

       Furthermore,     the    Manual      on     Position     Classification             and
Compensation issued by the DBM provides that higher local
governments (such as the City Government of Naga) are not
precluded to undertake projects in the barangay which may
include the payment of honoraria to its volunteer workers.

         SECTION 4.        ENGAGEMENT OF VOLUNTEERS - The engagement                             of
Volunteers            Workers    who      will     assist      in     the        solid        waste
management efforts of the City within their respective
barangays shall be facilitated by the Solid Waste Management
Office (SWMO) of the City Government of Naga which shall:
   a. Identify the necessary number of volunteers required
      for   each  barangay   depending   on   the  Barangay's
      population, area, and volume of solid waste collection,
         among other factors;
   b. Coordinate  jointly   with  the  Punong   Barangays  and
      Barangay Kagawad Chairperson on Environment for the
      engagement of competent people and submit their names
      to the Solid Waste Management Office. The incumbent
      Barangay Environment Monitors of the 27 Barangays and/or
         other         volunteer        workers        involved        in        solid        waste
         management          efforts          shall    have        preference            in     the
         engagement;
   c. Conduct the training and orientation of the engaged
      Barangay   Volunteers   on   the  proper management  and
      collection   of   solid    wastes  in   their respective
      barangays; and
   d. Be responsible in preparing the Engagement Contracts of
      the Barangay Volunteers and facilitate the submission
      of accomplishment reports, daily time records, and other
      documents which may be necessary in the processing of
         their        honoraria;
   e. Provide for the tools, equipment, materials, safety
      apparatus, and other assistance which may be necessary
      for the performance of responsibility of the appointed
         volunteers;
       f. Solicit the assistance of other City Government Offices,
          National Government Agencies, and NGOs to help ensure
          cleanliness of the Barangay surroundings which will be
         assigned to them;

         SECTION 5.        RESPONSIBILITIES           OF THE VOLUNTEER WORKERS                   ON

SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT. The Barangay Solid Waste Management
Volunteers, in coordination with the Punong Barangays and
Chairpersons of the Committee on Environment, shall be the
focal persons of the City Solid Waste Management Office in
ensuring the:
   a. Cleanliness and maintenance of barangay surroundings
      with major focus on roads, streets, drainage canals and
         existing bodies of water;
   b. Compliance to and enforcement of waste segregation
      policies and the functionality of the barangay materials
      recovery facility (MRF);
   c. Timely collection and transfer                          of    recyclables           to    the
         Barangay's        MRF and       the   solid    and    other      residual        wastes
         from     the     community's          collection          area     to    the     City's
         Sanitary Landfill;             and
   d. Effectiveness                of      waste        composting               efforts         by
         coordinating with the SWMO in the conduct of trainings,
         seminars,        and the       distribution of        information materials
         relative to proper solid waste management.

              In     addition,                 the     Barangay           Volunteer      Workers         shall     be
     deputized to issue citation tickets to violators of City or
     Barangay Ordinances and other issuances and shall also be
     entitled to the appropriate incentives provided in City
     Ordinance             2004-046.


              SECTION               6.     HONORARIA           AND        INCENTIVES.         The    volunteers
     workers engaged under this ordinance shall be entitled                                                  to    an

     Honoraria in the amount of Three Thousand (3,000.00)                                                    Pesos

     per    month.             In        addition,       the        Solid Waste          Management         Office
     shall     establish                  an   awards        and    incentives        scheme        to   annually
     recognize good performance and compliance of barangays                                                       and

     communities with pertinent environmental laws and issuances.

              SECTION               7.    MONITORING          AND     EVALUATION.         The       Solid    Waste
     Management Board shall be on top of monitoring and evaluating
     the effective implementation of the provisions of this
     Ordinance. An Annual Report shall be submitted to the City
     Mayor and the Sangguniang Panlungsod not later than the first
     quarter of every year.

          SECTION  7.   FUNDING.  The  necessary   funds  for  the
     implementation of this Ordinance shall be incorporated in the
     investment plan and budget of the Solid Waste Management
     Office and the City's budget beginning FY 2026.

          SECTION 8. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. Should any portion of
     this ordinance be declared unconstitutional or invalid, the
     other parts which are not thereby affected shall remain in
     full     force        and           effect.

k)            SECTION 9.                 EFFECTIVITY.          This ordinance shall be effected
