                                     MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 10-54-08

        BE IT ORDAINED by the 10th Sangguniang Bayan of Bayog, Zamboanga del Sur on
its 52nd Regular Session held at the Municipal Session Hall on August 11, 2008 at 9:00 a.m.


SECTION 1. TITLE - AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING ALL PREGNANT WOMEN TO HAVE
                   DELIVERIES/CHILDBIRTH ATTENDED BY SKILLED HEALTH
                   PROFESSIONAL OR TRAINED BARANGAY HEALTH WORKER
                   (BHW) AND PENALIZING VIOLATIONS THEREOF.


SECTION 2. SCOPE - Any pregnant woman who gives birth within the jurisdiction of the municipality of
                    Bayog, Zamboanga del Sur.


SECTION 3. PURPOSE
             1. To decrease maternal and neonatal mortality and morbidity due to inadequate skills
                and/or training of birth attendants.
             2. To increase childbirths attended by skilled Health Professionals.
             3. To encourage and increase facility-based deliveries.


SECTION 4. PENAL CLAUSE

                 Any pregnant woman who delivers without the attendance of a skilled health professional
or trained BHW shall be penalized as follows:
             st
           1 Offense : Reprimand both pregnant woman & untrained Birth attendant
            nd
           2 Offense : Fine of not more than Five Hundred Pesos .
            rd
           3 Offense: Fine of not less than Five Hundred Pesos but not more than One thousand
                           Pesos and non-registration of the newborn child/children.


SECTION 5. REPEALING CLAUSE - Any ordinance and existing rules inconsistent with this Ordinance
shall be repealed or amended accordingly.


SECTION EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect after three (3) consecutive weeks of publication
in a newspaper of General Circulation in the Province of Zamboanga del Sur.
