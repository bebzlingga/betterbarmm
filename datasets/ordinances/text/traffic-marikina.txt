                          ARTICLE 1 - GENERAL PROVISIONS

Section l.Title - This Ordinance shall be known and cited as ---

       "THE 2015 TRAFFIC MANAGEMENT CODE OF MARIKINA CITY"


Section 2. Declaration of Policy. - It is hereby the declared policy of the City Government of
Marikina that;
   (a) The flow of people and goods through the road network shall be as efficient, safe
       unhampered and orderly as possible for the economic and social vitality and viabitity of
       the city;
   (b) Urban road space is a scarce commodity the compelling use of which must be allocated
       for the greatest good and the greatest number through judicious, fair, participatory and
       informed traffi c management system;
   (c) Traffic problems and issues must be resolved in a rational manner, guided by facts and
       shaped through consultation, collaboration, and coordination with the surrounding cities
       and municipalities comprising with the Metropolitan Manila Development Authority as
       well as national agencies, like the Department of Public Works and Highways and the
       Department of Transportation and Communication;
   (d) The public has the right to be informed prior, and to participate in the formulation of an1,
       measures that may affect their community and travelling habits;

Section 3. Scope of Application   -
   (a) This Ordinance shall provide for efficient and effective traffic rules and regulations on all
       kinds of road in the City whether national or local in classification; for the management
       of traffic and safer use of roads, streets, avenues, highways and alleys of Marikina City
       by all types of motor vehicles, tricycles, bicycles, pedicabs, and other forms of
        conveyances, whether public or private; for the designation of vehicle stops, transport
        terminals and parking zones in public places; as well as, for pedestrian discipline: and in
        general, such other rules and regulations hereinafter promulgated in furtherance of an
        optimum utilization ofthe road network in the City, where the context applies;
    (b) All the applicable provisions of R.A. No. 4136, otherwise known as the "The Land
        Transportation and Traffic Codes of the Philippines", the "Public Service Law", the
        implementing rules and regulations prescribed by the Land Transportation Commission
        of the Department of Transportation and Communication, are all likewise in this
        Ordinance.

         ARTICLE II TRAFFIC CONTROL SIGNAL, SIGNS AND SIGNAGES

Section 5. Erection and Interference with Traffic Control Items        -
   (a) No person shall, except when duly authorized by the proper authority, erect, establish or
       display on any road or in the view of any person on any road, or interfere with, alter to
       take down, any traffic control signal, signs or signages.
   (b) No person shall erect, establish, place, maintain, or display on any road or in the view of
       any person on any road anything which purports to be or is an imitation or similar to any
       traffic control sign or item, which interfere with the effectiveness of or prevents an
       approaching driver from clearly distinguishing the whole part ofany traffic control item,
       or distracts his attention from any traffic sign or item.

Section 6. Limits on Operation of Signs - Any sign associated with a "No Parking Area", "No
Waiting Area", or "Parking Area", or any sign of a kind referred to in Section 10 shall be limited
in its operation and effect in respect of days, periods of the day, classes of vehicle or
circumstances to the extent (ifany) shown on the sign.

Section 7. Traffic Control Signs or ltems to be Operative    -

       Sections with respect to dimensions, shape, color, position, direction, single or any other
       features of traffic-control signs or items or any kind shall be deemed to be traffic-control
       sign or item ofthat kind.

                        ARTICLE III - GENERAL DRIVING RULES

Section 11. Keeping as Far Right as Practicable. - Unless otherwise provided for in this Code,
a driver shall keep his vehicle so close as practicable to the right boundary of the thoroughfare,
except, where there are two or more lanes marked on the thoroughfare available exclusively for
the traffic in the direction in which he is travelling.

Section l2.Obstruction to Driver's View or Driving Mechanism           -
   (a) No person shall drive a vehicle when it is over-loaded, or when there are in the front seat
       such numbers of person, exceeding three, or as to interfere with the drivers control over
       the driving mechanism of the vehicle.
   (b) No passenger in a vehicle shalt ride in such a position as to interfere with the driver's
       view ahead or to the sides, or to interfere with his control over the driving mechanism of
        the vehicle.

shall keep to his right relative to that vehicle.

driver of any vehicle involved in an accident resulting to injury or death of any person, or
damage to a vehicle which is driving or attended by any person shall immediately stop and
remain at the scene of the accident until he has fulfilled the requirements of Section 69 of this
Ordinance. Every step must be taken to ensure that such stoppage ofthe vehicle is made without
obstructing vehicular traffi c.

     (a) The driver of any vehicle involved in an accident resulting to injury or death of any
         person shall, immediately by the quickest or faster means of communication' give
         notice ofsuch accident to the nearest police precinct or local traffic authority.
     (b) The driver of any vehicle involved in property damage, shall make an agreement with
         the person whose property is damage, that is' as to the amount of property damage
         sustained. However, if no agreement can be reached between the two parties, the
         accident shall be reported immediately to the police department.
     (c) Every law enforcement officer, who's in the regular course of duty, investigate a motor

Section 74. Repair Shop/Garage to Report - The owner or person in charge of any repair shop
or garage to which motor vehicle brought showed evidence of having been involved in an
accident, in the commission of crime, or struck by a bultet, shall report to the nearest police
precinct within twenty four (24) hours after such motor vehicle is received, giving the engine
number, plate number, the name and address of the owner or driver of such vehicle, and a
description ofthe suspicious or unusual circumstances. The shop/garage owner shall be imposed
with the maximum penalty of P5, 000.00 if he failed to report to the police authorities about the
crime-involved vehicle.

Section 75. Accident Reports: confidential - All accident reports in writing made by the
person involved in the accident or by repair shops/garages, shall be for the confidential use of the
police department or other City agencies concemed who have asked for the records for accident
prevention purposes, except that the police depa(ment may disclose the identity of a person
involved in an accident to the aggrieved party when his identity is not clear or when such person
denies his involvement in the accident. The appropriate police authorities shall, upon written
demand by the aggrieved party or by the Court of Justice, issue a certification of said report of
accident.
