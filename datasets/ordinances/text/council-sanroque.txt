       WHEREAS, Article ll, Section 23 of the Philippine Constitution states that the "State shall encourage
non-governmental, community-based or sectoral organizations that promote the welfare ofthe nation". This
is further asserted in Article Xlll, Section 15 and 16 that the State shall respect the role and rights of
independent people's organizations in the pursuit of their collective interests and aspirations and ensure their
effective and reasonable participation at all levels of r;ocial, political, and economic decision-making,

        WHEREAS, this participation further affirmed in the Local Govemment Code, specifically in Ailcle
62, Rule Xlll, lmplementing Rules and Regulations of the Local Govemment Code which provides that local
govemment units shall promote the establishment and operation of non{overnment organizations, people's
organizations and private sector to make them active partner in the pursuit of local autonomy, and to directly
involve them in planning and implementation of programs and project of LGUs;

       WHEREAS, by far, the most concrete expression of people's participation in govemance is the role
played by the civil society organizations (CSOs), non-government organization (NGOs), and people's
organizations (POs), in the so called local special boriies, such as the development council, which according
to the Local govemment Code has a crucial role: formulate plans that would determine what development
projects should be pursued and how these would be financed, among others;
