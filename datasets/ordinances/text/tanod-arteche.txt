Series of 2025

AN ORDINANCE GRANTING MONTHLY INCENTIVES TO BARANGAY TANODS IN THE
MUNICIPALITY OF ARTECHE, EASTERN SAMAR, TO AUGMENT THE HONORARIUM
PROVIDED BY THEIR RESPECTIVE BARANGAYS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
THEREFOR

Sponsored by:
Hon. Junel T, Capati

WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 389 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local
Government Code of 1991, barangays are mandated to maintain peace and order and provide

for effective mechanisms to ensure the safety and security of the community;

WHEREAS, Barangay Tanods serve as frontliners in maintaining peace and order, assisting
in crime prevention, emergency response, and the enforcement of barangay ordinances and

national laws at the community level;

WHEREAS, Barangay Tanods perform their duties often beyond regular hours and in high-
risk situations, without the benefits and privileges enjoyed by regular government personnel; i

WHEREAS, under Section 391 (a) (16) of the Local Government Code, the Sangguniang
Barangay, as the legislative body of the barangay, shall provide for the organization of
community brigade, barangay tanod, or community service unit as may be necessary;

WHEREAS, Section 393 (d) of the LGC, mandates the granting of insurance and other benefits
to duly appointed Barangay Tanods chargeable against barangay and municipal funds.

WHEREAS, providing regular incentives to Barangay Tanods is a form of recognition for their
invaluable service and a means to motivate and sustain their commitment and efficiency;

WHEREAS, it is imperative to institutionalize a fair and consistent incentive system for
Barangay Tanods to enhance their morale, improve performance, and ensure continued

' service to the barangay;

NOW THEREFORE, be it ordained by the Sangguniang Bayan of Arteche, Eastern Samar, in

session assembled, that:
SECTION 1. Short Title. This Ordinance shall be known as the “Barangay Tanod {

Incentives Ordinance of Arteche.”

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. It is the policy of the Municipality of Arteche to recognize
and support the role of Barangay Tanods in maintaining peace and order, and to provide
appropriate incentives, training, and recognition in line with RA 7160 and DILG issuances.

SECTION 3. Purpose. This Ordinance aims to empower and provide incentives to members
of the Barangay Tanod to encourage, inspire, and motivate them to sustain and further
improve the quality of their services to the community.

SECTION 4. Coverage. This Ordinance shall apply to all duly appointed and accredited
members of the Barangay Tanod Brigade in every barangay within the Municipality of
Arteche, Eastern Samar, who are actively performing their duties and responsibilities in
maintaining peace and order in their respective areas of jurisdiction.

SECTION 5. Number of Barangay Tanods. Each barangay in the Municipality of Arteche
shall organize and maintain a Barangay Tanod Brigade composed of not more than twenty (20)
Tanods, who shall be entitled to insurance coverage and other benefits during their term of
service, in accordance with Section 393(d) of Republic Act No. 7160 and pertinent DILG
guidelines.

SECTION 6. Accreditation of Barangay Tanods. Only duly appointed Barangay Tanods,

officially endorsed by their respective Punong Barangays and confirmed by a Barangay
Resolution, shall be entitled to incentives upon accreditation by the Tanod Accreditation
Board created under this Ordinance.

The Accreditation Board shall evaluate and validate all appointments, maintain the master list
of accredited Tanods, and submit the same to the Sangguniang Bayan for notation.

Tanods shall be granted an initial monthly incentive of Two Hundred Pesos (P200.00) by the
Municipal Government to augment the honorarium they receive from their respective
barangays, subject to the availability of funds and auditing rules.

SECTION 10, Source of Funds. An initial amount of P1,000,000.00 shall be appropriated from
the General Fund under the Peace and Order and Public Safety Fund of the Office of the Mayor
for the implementation of this Ordinance and shall be included in each year's annual budget.

incorporated in the succeeding annual budgets thereafter.
