Municipal Ordinance No. 2022 - 14
AN ORDINANCE GOVERNING THE DEVELOPMENT, MANAGEMENT AND
UTILIZATION OF THE CO-MANAGED FORESTS AND FORESTLAND OF THE
MUNICIPALITY OF OPOL, MISAMIS ORIENTAL

Introduced by Hon. Danilo O. Daroy

WHEREAS, Section 3(i) of the Local Government Code expressly states that local
government units shall share with the national government the responsibility in the
management and maintenance of ecological balance within their territorial jurisdiction,
subject to the provisions of the Code and national policies;

WHEREAS, Section 17 (b) (2) (ii) of the same Code states that pursuant to national
policies and subject to supervision, control and review of the DENR, implementation of
community-based forestry projects which include integrated social forestry programs and
similar projects; management and control of communal forests with an area not exceeding
fifty (50) square kilometers; establishment of tree parks, greenbelts, and similar forest
development projects;

WHEREAS, under Section 1 of the DENR DAO No. 92-30, the transfer and

implementation of certain DENR functions devolved to the LGUs shall be governed by the
following policies:

a) The DENR shall remain as the primary government agency responsible for the
conversation, management, protection, development and proper use of the country’s
environment and natural resources and the promotion of sustainable development;
b) The LGUs shall have with the national government, particularly the DENR, the
responsibility in the sustainable management and development of the environment
and natural resources within their territorial jurisdictions;
c) The implementation of the devolved functions by the municipalities and cities and
enforcement of laws, rules and regulations pertaining to the devolved functions, as
“ee provided for in the Local Government Code, shall be pursuant to national policies and
subject to supervision, control, and review of the DENR. Provided that, when
necessary, the concerned provinces, cities and municipalities shall enter into
administrative arrangements to effectively and efficiently enforce the laws, rules and
regulations pertaining to the devolved functions; and
d) Programs, projects or activities which are wholly or partially funded from foreign
sources or those included as agency budgetary program, project, or item under the
General Appropriations Act, pertinent EOs and other special laws, including the
Comprehensive Agrarian Reform Program (CARP), shall be exempt from devolution,
thus retained by the DENR.

WHEREAS, Section 1.3 of the DENR-DILG JMC No. 98-01 likewise provides that
comprehensive land use and forest land use plans are important tools in the holistic and

together with other government agencies shall undertake forest land use planning as an
integral activity of comprehensive land use planning to determine the optimum and balanced
use of natural resources to support local, regional and national growth and development;

WHEREAS, Section 1.4 of the same Circular further emphasized that to fully prepare
the LGUs to undertake their shared responsibilities in the sustainable management of forest
land resources, the DENR, in coordination with DILG, shall enhance the capacities of the
LGUs in the various aspects of forest management. Initially, the DENR shall coordinate,
guide and train the LGUs in the management of the devolved functions. As the LGUs’
capacity in forest management is enhanced, the primary tasks in the management of
devolved functions shall be performed by the LGUs and the role of the DENR becomes
assistive and coordinative;

WHEREAS, Section 2 of the DENR-DILG JMC No. 2003-01 clarifies that the
partnership among the DENR, DILG, and LGU shall be evidenced by a Memorandum of
Agreement (MOA) to be signed by the concerned DENR, DILG, LGU officials and their
Leagues. The MOA shall specify the roles and functions of the parties, their internal rules of
business, the composition, roles and functions of the Technical Working Group, and their
commitments to the partnership;

approval of by DENR shall be manifested through a MOA between the DENR and the LGU.
The MOA shall provide for the commitments of the two parties toward the full
implementation of the FLUP;

SECTION 2. Objectives. - The following are the objectives:
2.1 To improve the management of Opol Co-Managed Areas;

2.2 To increase forest cover by rehabilitating grasslands, denuded and other degraded
areas;

2.3 To increase supply of food, agricultural and industrial products and energy in order to
provide additional sources of income and livelihood to the people and promote economic
upliftment;

2.4 To utilize co-management area for potential sustainable project without destroying or
limiting the forest of the Municipality of Opol;

2.5 To generate additional sources of funds that can be used by the Local Government Unit

of Opol for improving environmental management and conservation activities in the
municipality;

2.6 To provide security of tenure to claimants or stakeholders of the Co-Management Area;
2.7 To set-up mechanisms for convergence of project and development players;
2.8 To institutionalize public and private sector partnership in developing forestlands; and

2.9 To establish a partnership between the Local Government Unit of Opol and the DENR-
10 as well as among the participating forestland users in promoting the sustainable use of
the Co-Management Area.

SECTION 3. Definition of Terms - For the purpose of this Ordinance, the following terms
shall apply:

1. Agro-forestry - a suitable management of farm, which increases overall production that
combines agricultural crops, tree crops and forest plants and/or animals simultaneously or
sequentially and applies management practices which are compatible with the cultural
patterns of the local populations.

2. Alienable and Disposable Lands - refers to those lands of the public domain which
have been the subject of the present system of classification and declared as not needed for
forest purposes.

3. Bio-diversity - is also known as biological diversity; the variability among living
organisms from all sources including terrestrial, marine and other aquatic ecosystems and
the ecological complexes of which they are apart; this includes diversity within species,
between species and of the eco-system.

4.FLUP Agreement - the Co-Management Agreement by and between the LGU Opol and
the DENR-10 to jointly manage and develop natural resources covering 15,362 hectares of
forests and forestland in the Municipality of Opol.

5. Critical Watershed - is a drainage area of a river system supporting existing and
proposed hydro-electric power, irrigation works, or domestic water facilities needing
immediate protection or rehabilitation.

6. Farmlands - refer to the area devoted primarily for the purpose of agro forestry
production.

7. Forestland - include the public forest, the permanent forest or forest reserves, and forest _

8. Forest Product - means timber, pulpwood, firewood, bark, tree top, resin, gum, wood, oil,
honey, beeswax, rattan, or other forest growth such as grass, shrub, and flowering plant, the
associated water, fish, scenic, historical, recreational, geological resources in forest lands,
and other forest products.

9. Game Refuge or Bird Sanctuary - refers to a forest land designated for the protection of
birds and fish and closed to hunting and fishing in order that the excess population may flow
and restock surroundings areas.

10. Industrial Tree Plantation - refers to any forest land extensively planted to crops
primarily to supply the raw material requirements of existing or proposed wood processing
plants and related industries.

41. Joint DILG-DENR Memorandum Circular 2003-01 - refers to the circular signed by the
DENR and DILG for strengthening and institutionalizing DENR-DILG-LGU partnership on
devolved and other forest management functions.

12. Kaingin - refers to a portion of the forest land which is subjected to shifting and/or
permanent slash-and-burn cultivation.

13. Lease - is a privilege granted by the State to a person to occupy and possess, in
consideration of specified rental, any forest land of the public domain in order to undertake
any authorized activity therein.

14. License Agreement - is a privilege granted by the State to a person to utilize forest
resources within any forest land with the right of possession and occupation thereof to the
exclusion of others, except the government, but with the corresponding obligation to
develop, protect and rehabilitate the same in accordance with the terms and conditions set
forth in said agreement.

15. License - is a privilege granted by the State to a person to utilize forest resources within
any forest land, without any right of occupation and possession over the same, to the
exclusion of others, or establish and operate a wood-processing plant, or conduct any
activity involving the utilization of any forest resources.

16. Management Sub-Agreement - refers to the agreement entered into by and between
the Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Council (MENRC)/Steering Committee of
Opol Co-Management Area and a qualified applicant, which grants to the latter the right to
develop, utilize and manage an area for specific purposes, consistent with the principle of
sustainable development and in accordance with the Forest Land Use Plan (FLUP) (also
referred to as Agro-Forestry Framework Plan or Farm Plan) approved in writing by the
MENRC/Steering Committee.

17. Mineral Lands - refer to those lands of the public domain which have been classified as
such by the Secretary of Environment and Natural Resources in accordance with prescribed
and approved criteria, guidelines, and procedure.

18. Multiple-use - is the harmonized utilization of the land, soil, water, wildlife, recreation
value, grass and timber of forest lands.

19. National Park - refers to a forest land reservation essentially of primitive or wilderness
character which has been withdrawn from settlement or occupancy and set aside as such
exclusively to preserve the scenery, the natural and historic objects and the wild animals or
plants therein, and to provide enjoyment of those features in such a manner as will leave
them unimpaired for future generations.

20. Permanent Forest or Forest Reserves - refers to those lands of the public domain
which have been the subject of the present system of classification and declared as needed

for forest purposes.

or conversion of logs and other forest raw materials into lumber, veneer, plywood,

26. Property Rights - in general, property rights are the set of rights belonging to a person
or group of persons over a particular property. Property rights also refer to a stream of
benefits from a given set of property, particularly land and its resources, such as minerals,
forest products, wildlife, water, fish, marine products, and the like. Rights are
instrumentalities by which society controls and orders human interdependence and resolve
the question of who gets what. Set of rules that describe the relationship of one person to
another with respect to a resource or any line of action.

27. Public Forest - is the mass of lands of the public domain which has not been the
subject to the person system of classification for the determination of which lands are
needed for forest purposes and which are not.

28. Resource Use Plan - a plan prepared by an applicant for agro-forestry agreement
which describes how assets within the area subject of application will be developed and
managed.

29. MENRC/Steering Committee - refers to the Municipal Environment and Natural
Resources Council of Opol, a multi-sectoral body that serves as the governing body of the
Co-Management Area in accordance with the provisions of the Co-Management Agreement.
It is chaired by the Local Chief Executive and Co-Chaired by DENR-CENRO Initao.

30. Sustainable Agro-forestry - utilization of resources in a way and at a rate that would
maintain their potential to meet the needs and aspirations of the present and future
generations.

31. Sustainable Development - meeting the needs of today without compromising the
ability of future generations to meet their.

32. Tree Farm - refers to any small forest land or tract of land purposely planted to tree
crops.

33. Watershed - is a land area drained by a stream or fixed body of water and its tributaries
having common outlet for surface run-off.

34. Watershed Reservation - is a forest land reservation established to protect or improve
the conditions of the water yield thereof or reduce sedimentation.

SECTION 5. Size of the Area. - There is no minimum area that may be covered by a
management sub-agreement; provided that the maximum area for an individual/single family
shall not exceed five (5) hectares nor shall any such individual/single family hold more than
one management sub-agreement; provided further, that an
association/cooperative/corporation may hold more than one sub agreement simultaneously
but the maximum aggregate total size of the area shall be determined and approved by the
MENRC/Technical Working Committee.

SECTION 6. Qualified Applicants. - The following are qualified to apply:

6.1 Individuals/single family units who are Filipino citizens, of legal age or head of the family
and presently occupying, cultivating and/or residing within or adjacent to the CMA area.
Priority shall be given to the individual/family who have been staying in the area including
claimants, as stated in the above-mentioned Claims Map, before the effective date of this
Ordinance will also be given priority; applicants residing outside the co-managed area of the
municipality shall be entertained; provided, further, that the area applied does not exceed
five (5) hectares.

6.2 Cooperatives, associations and/or corporations whose members or BOD are Filipino
citizens and preferably residents of the Municipality of Opol, duly registered with the
Cooperative Development Authority or Securities and Exchange Commission and duly
accredited by Local Government Unit of Opol, as the case may be; provided that
cooperatives, associations and/or corporations applying for more than one (1) agreement
must show proof of financial and technical capacity to develop the areas applied for in
conformity with the approved CRMP (Community Resource Management Plan).

the IPR orientation seminar.

collaboration with concerned occupants, Purok Leaders, Barangay Chairmen. As part of the

Interview (KIl). The result of field verification shall be reflected in the Individual Claims Form
(IPR Form No. 2) with corresponding signatures of key persons (e.g. Sitio leader, Barangay

Chairman, PO Leader). The claims maps shall be validated and duly signed by the MENRO
staff.

9.7 Endorsement by the MENR Office. Upon receipt of the application forms and validated
claims map, the documents including the sub-agreements duly signed by the applicant shall
be endorsed by the MENRO to the Mayor/MENRC for approval of the application.

application documents including the sub-agreement, the MENRC shall, within 15 working

SECTION 10. Secretariat Responsibility of the MENRO.- The MENRO shall be
responsible for providing Secretariat services to the MENRC/Technical Working Committee
and, as such, be primarily responsible and accountable for the proper receipt, recording,
and safe keeping of all documents and official transactions of the MENRC.

SECTION 11. A Special Account for Forest Co-Management Fund. - All fees collected in
accordance with Section 8 of this Ordinance shall be credited to this account, including all
other fees and charges that may hereafter be authorized by Ordinance to be imposed and
collected from any undertaking associated with the Co-Management Area.

SECTION 12. Integration of Agro Forestry Agreement Holders into Larger
Organizations. - Holders of agro forestry management agreements shall be encouraged to
confederate into cooperatives, associations and/or federations to enable them to avail of the
benefits.

The MENRC and the Project Management Unit, in collaboration with other departments of
the Local Government Unit of Opol, national agencies, and other organizations shall provide
the necessary technical assistance, capacity building through skills training, and
organizational management, as well as linkaging for access to markets and social services,
livelihood and financing.

The MENRC and Project Management Unit shall explore ways and means to develop
incentives in ways that enable sub-agreement holders to develop and manage their
awarded areas; provided that the Local Government Unit of Opol and all its departments
shall ipso facto prioritize holders of valid sub-agreements in the provision of such incentives,
awards, grants, subsidies and technical assistance benefits.

SECTION 13. Grounds for Cancellation. - The MENRC/Steering Committee reserve the
right to cancel any management sub-agreement for cause or when the public interest so
requires. The Sub-agreement may be cancelled by the MENRC/Steering Committee for
violation or non-compliance with any of the terms and conditions stipulated in this
Ordinance, after the observance of the due Process of Law.

SECTION 14. Management of the Co-Management Area. - In accordance with the
provisions of the Co-Management Agreement, the MENRC/Steering Committee shall serve
as the governing body responsible for the overall management of the Co- Management Area
with such powers and responsibilities as stipulated in the Co Management Agreement. To
ensure its effective operation, the MENRC/Technical Working Committee may, from time to
time, agree in a meeting called for the purpose, to reconstitute its membership and authorize
the Municipal Mayor to issue the appropriate executive order therefore; provided, that its
multi-sectoral character shall at all times be maintained and enhanced.

Further, in accordance with the Co-Management Agreement the Project Management Unit
(PMU) shall be directly responsible and accountable to the MENRC/Steering Committee for
carrying out its policies and decisions, including the day-to-day implementation of the
approved work and financial plans and activities.

The Mayor shall designate or otherwise appoint all the officers and staff of the PMU duly
recommended by the MENRC/Steering Committee; provided, that all PMU personnel shall
be responsible and accountable to, and their performance regularly reviewed and evaluated
by, the MENRC/Steering Committee; provided, further, that, the promotion, replacement or
removal of any PMU personnel or staff supported from the special account cited in Section

11 of this Ordinance shall be subject to the unanimous approval of the MENRC/Steering
Committee in a resolution duly passed for the purpose.

SECTION 15. Register of Available Areas. - The Project Management Unit shall maintain
a Register containing the location and description of all areas that have been determined to
be suitable and available for issuance of management sub-agreements, including a general
prescription for the management and development of the area as well as restrictions as may
be necessary. The register shall be accessible to local communities, government agencies
and other stakeholders at any reasonable time. The right of local stakeholders to ask for and
receive information on the Co Management Area shall not be impaired.

SECTION 16. Management of the Awarded Areas. - Responsibility and accountability for
proper management of the awarded area shall be achieved through the issuance of
management sub-agreements or the Opol Agro Forestry Land Management Agreement
(ALMA) which shall have a duration of 25 years or until such time that the Co-Management
Agreement shall have expired or renewed.

The Management Sub-Agreement shall be issued in the name of the applicant, provided,
that in the case of married individuals, it shall be issued in the names of both spouses.

The IPR holder may opt to enter into a development contract or investment agreement with
private sector (whether individual, association, cooperative or corporation) for the purpose of
developing portion of the awarded IPR areas. The MENRC/Steering Committee must
conform to the agreement to ensure compliance with the indicative resource management
plan and at the same time address social equity and social justice.

The Management Sub-Agreement shall not be transferred, conveyed, leased nor sold, in
whole or in part, to any person, cooperative or association except upon prior approval of the
MENRC/Steering Committee for some compelling reason/s, otherwise the MENRC/Steering
Committee will take over the area.

All improvements introduced in the area subject for Management Sub-Agreement shall be
allowed for issuance of tax declaration by the Local Assessor, provided that the declarant's
name is Republic of the Philippines and the beneficial user thereof is the IPR holder.

SECTION 17. Monitoring and Evaluation of Intellectual Property Rights (IPR)
Holders/IPR Areas. The MENRC/Steering Committee through the IPR Committee shall
conduct a regular performance-based monitoring of all tenure/IPR holders. The area
development plan of IPR holders shall be used in the conduct of said Monitoring &
Evaluation activities. In addition, the MENRC/Steering Committee shall formulate an LGU-
based Monitoring & Evaluation system to monitor the overall implementation of the IRMP
including the impacts of developments of IPR/tenured areas.

SECTION 18. Promotion of Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) Areas. In the absence of
investors and to generate investments in the uplands, the MENRO, in collaboration with
various LGU's and/or stakeholders shall conduct regular investment forum or participate in
similar activities or other activities that will generate the interest and commitment of private
sector to invest in IPR areas and/or upland communities in Opol.

SECTION 19. Financing Mechanism. The funds for implementation of this ordinance shall
be sourced from the general fund and co-management account/trust fund. Funds can also
be sourced from any donor, agencies or individuals in the forms of projects or donations in
cash or in kind. Filing fee, environmental user's fee including cash donations shall be
deposited to the co-management account/trust fund.
