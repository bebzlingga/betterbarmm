 AN ORDINANCE INSTITUTIONALIZING THE DAMGO SA KABUKIRAN PROGRAM OF THE MUNICIPALITY
                             OF MAGSAYSAY, DAVAO DEL SUR.

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Bayan of Magsaysay, Davao del Sur in session assembled that:
Section 1. SHORT TITLE - This ordinance shall be cited, for brevity, as “ Institutionalizing the Damgo Sa
Kabukiran Program of the Municipality of Magsaysay, Davao del Sur.”
Section 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY- It is hereby declared a policy of the Municipality of Magsaysay,
Davao del Sur that:.

a. The Municipality of Magsaysay underscore the value of Trees and other Perennial Crops and its
importance in keeping the environment, balance and upland biodiversity, hence promote and advance
programs and mechanism on massive reforestation.

       b. The Municipality of Magsaysay recognizes that each farmer has a fundamental and absolute
right to a healthful environment and that each one has responsibility to contribute to the preservation
and enhancement of the same.

      c. The Municipality of Magsaysay recognizes the importance of agriculture in making available
food and other products necessary to sustain and enhance human life, and in providing livelihood to a
major portion of the population.

       d. The Municipality shall also seek to promote environment-friendly, efficient and sustainable
farm practices; provide alternative recreation facilities and farm tourism activities for families, students
and other clientele; and promote health and wellness with high-quality farm-produced food.

       e. To maximize the benefits that can be derived from agriculture and tourism combined, it is
therefore the declared policy of the Municipality to provide the policy and enabling environment for the
encouragement, development and promotion of farm tourism in the municipality, province and the
country as a whole.

           e. There is a need to acquire a balance between population and resources use.

 Section 3. SCOPE- This ordinance covers all Sangguniang Barangays of this Municipality who intend to
 avail this program from the office of the Municipal Mayor.

 Section 4. PROGRAM- Damgo sa Kabukiran Program is hereby institutionalized in the Office of the
 Municipal Mayor as a long running program on the opening of access road in every Barangay and
 requiring the farmers of a massive planting of perennial crops, fruit-bearing trees and reforestation.
 Section 5. Mechanism- The program shall be implemented under the following mechanism;

       1. The Barangay Local Government Unit undertaking the opening of access road automatically
 participates in the “Damgo Sa Kabukiran Program”.

      2. The Office of the Municipal Engineer shall facilitate the program upon the approval of the
 Municipal Mayor.

       3. The Office of the Municipal Agriculturist shall facilitate the distribution of the planting materials
 covered by this program.

 Section 4. FUNDING- Funds necessary for the operation and implementation of the program shall be
 appropriated for the purpose.

 Section 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE- If any part of this Ordinance is declared not valid, unconstitutional or
 unlawful, such declaration shall not affect or impair the remaining provisions, sections or parts thereof,
 which shall remain or continue to be in full force and effect

 Section 5. EEFECTIVITY- This ordinance shall take effect upon its approval
 ENACTED this 27th of May, 2019.
