MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 2025-03
Series of 2025

AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING GUIDELINES FOR THE REGISTRATION AND
ACCREDITATION OF BARANGAY HEALTH WORKERS (BHWs) IN THE
MUNICIPALITY OF ARTECHE, EASTERN SAMAR, AND GRANTING OF
PERFORMANCE INCENTIVES TO QUALIFIED BHWs, APPROPRIATING FUNDS
THEREOF AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Sponsor: Hon. Eloise Melloney P. Evardone, RN, MAN
Chairman, Committee on Health & Sanitation

WHEREAS, Section 15, Article II of the 1987 Constitution of the Republic of the
Philippines mandates the State to protect and promote the right to health of the people
and instill health consciousness among them;

WHEREAS, Section 17 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local
Government Code of 1991, devolves the delivery of basic health services to Local
Government Units (LGUs), including programs for primary health care, maternal and
child health, and control of communicable and non-communicable diseases;

WHEREAS, Republic Act No. 7883, or the Barangay Health Workers’ Benefits and
Incentives Act of 1995, recognizes the indispensable role of Barangay Health Workers
(BHWs) in the delivery of basic health services at the grassroots level and provides
for incentives and benefits to encourage their continued service;

WHEREAS, DOH-DILG Joint Memorandum Circular No. 2023-001, with subject:
"Retention and Continued Service of Barangay Health Workers (BHWs)", establishes
policies to ensure protection and sustainability of the BHW workforce by
strengthening support mechanisms, providing incentives, and promoting long-term
engagement of accredited BHWs within the local health system;

WHEREAS, the Municipality of Arteche, Eastern Samar, recognizes the vital

|
contribution of BHWs in delivering essential health services, especially in
underserved communities, and seeks to institutionalize their registration,
accreditation, and provision of performance incentives to sustain their motivation and
commitment to public service;

WHEREAS, in pursuit of an efficient and well-organized local health workforce, the
municipality deems it necessary to establish clear guidelines for the recognition and
incentive system for BHWs, in line with national policies and local government
priorities;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED, by the Sangguniang Bayan of Arteche,

Eastern Samar, in session duly assembled, that the following guidelines be adopted
for the registration, accreditation, and granting of performance incentives to
Ze Barangay Health Workers (BHWs) in the municipality.

SECTION 1. Title. This ordinance shall be known as the BARANGAY HEALTH
WORKERS ORDINANCE of Arteche, Eastern Samar.

SECTION 2. Declaration of Policy. It is hereby declared a policy of the municipality
of Arteche, Eastern Samar to provide guidelines for the registration and accreditation
of Barangay Health Workers pursuant to RA 7883 and grant Performance Incentive to
Barangay Health Workers who continuously render basic health services in the
municipality of Arteche, Eastern Samar for a minimum of ten (10) years.

SECTION 3. Definition of Terms:

The following words, terms, and phrases enumerated hereunder and used in
this ordinance are understood to have the corresponding meaning indicated as
follows:

a. Barangay Health Worker (BHW) - refers to a person who has undergone
training programs under any accredited government and non-government
organization and who voluntarily renders primary health care services in the
community after having been accredited to function as such by the local health
board in accordance with the guidelines promulgated by the Department of
Health (DOR).

b. Barangay Health Officer (BHO)- is a designated position appointed by the
Punong Barangay separate from the Barangay Health Worker, that will render
primary health services in the Barangay.

c. Performance Incentive— is an incentive awarded to deserving BHW who

actively and regularly perform their duty for a minimum of ten (10) years upon
separation.

‘d. Local Health Board (LHB) — is the main advisory body on health of the LGU
created through the mandate of RA 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991.

e. Registration and Accreditation Committee (RAC) - A committee created by
the Local Health to facilitate the registration and accreditation of BHW

f. Certificate of Accreditation — is an official acknowledgement by the Local
Health Board of the merits of a BHW in meeting the standards prescribed by
existing laws.

SECTION 4. REGISTRATION AND ACCREDITATION. Barangay Health Workers
shall be entitled to the benefits and incentives specified under this Ordinance,
provided that said BHWs are registered and accredited by the Local Health Board.

To facilitate registration and accreditation of BHWs, the LHB shall create a BHW
Registration and Accreditation Committee (BHW-RAC) in accordance with the
Implementing Rules and Regulations of Republic Act No. 7883, which is composed of
the following:

1. The Municipal Health Officer (MHO) as Chairperson;

2. A representative of a non-government organization/people’s organization

implementing community-based health or health-related programs in the

SECTION 5. BHW Registration Qualification Standard. Barangay Health Workers
must possess the following qualifications:

1. Must be 18-59 years old ;

2. Atleast High School Level;

3. Bonafide resident of the Barangay for at least one (1) year prior to
application;

4, Have rendered voluntary health care service for at least one (1) year as
certified by the Rural Health Midwife (RHM) assigned in the Barangay;

5. Completed the basic training for BHWs conducted by an accredited
government agency or NGO;

6. Mentally and physically fit;

SECTION 6. Qualifications for Accreditation — In order to qualify for accreditation,
the applicant BHW must;

1. Be registered Barangay Health Worker; and

2. Have actively rendered voluntary primary health services in his/her locality
for at least three (3) years immediately prior to the filing of the application
for accreditation.

SECTION 7. Term of office — Although this Ordinance acknowledges the right
supervision of the Barangay Officials over the BHW, still NO BHW shall be removed
from the service by mere order of the Barangay Officials without compliance with due
process requirements of notice and hearing. He/She cannot be removed without valid
cause and after compliance with the notice and hearing requirements of the LHB.

SECTION 8. Grounds for Termination as Barangay Health Worker — The LHB shall
SS determine the basis of the termination of the BHW on any of the following grounds:

Insubordination

Inactive service for 3 months

Failure to submit reports and accomplishments.
Transfer of residence

Engaging in unethical behavior

Found guilty of a criminal offense

SECTION 10. Computation of Performance Incentive. All accredited Barangay
Health Workers (BHWs), who have continuously served and performed their duties
for a minimum of five (5) years, shall receive, upon separation and end of service,
regardless of their age, the cash incentive stated below:

Length of Service Benefit
5 years (minimum years of service) P2,000.00 + additional P200 per year in
excess of 5 years
10 years and above P4,000.00 + additional P200.00 per year

in excess of 10 years

SECTION 11. Requirement of Claims. The following documents shall be required
for the grant of the retirement benefit.

a. Certification of Registration/Accreditation;
b. Service Record from the Barangay Health Center or RHU

SECTION 12. Appropriation of Fund. The funds necessary for the grant of

performance incentives for qualified BHWs of the municipality shall be included in the
annual budget under the Gender and Development Fund.

SECTION 13. Review by the Local Health Board. Every incentive or benefit for
BHW requiring the expenditure of local funds shall be reviewed and approved by the
Local Health Board to ensure that only qualified BHWs shall avail of the incentive.

SECTION 14. Separability Clause. The provisions of this ordinance are hereby
declared separable. If one or more provisions are held invalid or unconstitutional, the
remainder, or any provision hereof not affected, shall remain valid and effective.

SECTION 15. Repealing Clause. All ordinances, rules, and regulations or any part

thereof, in conflict or inconsistent with any provisions of this ordinance are hereby
repealed or modified accordingly.

SECTION 16. Effectivity. This ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after

posting at three (3) conspicuous places in the municipality. :
