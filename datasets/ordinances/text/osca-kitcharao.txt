WHEREAS, forwarded to the Sangguniang Panlalawigan of Agusan del Norte for
review is Municipal Ordinance No. 41, series of 2015, dated July 27, 2015 from the
Sangguniang Bayan of Kitcharao, this province, entitled: “An Ordinance Creating the
Office of the Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA) headed by a Senior Citizen in the
Municipality of Kitcharao as Mandated by R.A. 9257”,

WHEREAS, the subject municipal ordinance was referred to the Committee on
Social Welfare and Development for study, evaluation and recommendation;

WHEREAS, in its objective of giving importance on the vital role of the elderly in
nation-building, the Sangguniang Bayan of Kitcharao enacted Municipal Ordinance No.
41, creating the Office of the Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA) in the municipality;

WHEREAS, cities and municipalities in the country are mandated to establish
an OSCA office in their respective localities as provided for under Section 6 of R.A.
9994, “An Act Granting Additional Benefits and Privileges to Senior Citizens, Further
Amending Republic Act No. 7432, as Amended, Otherwise Known as “An Act to
Maximize the Contribution of Senior Citizens, to National Building, Grant Benefits and
Special Privileges and for Other Purposes;

SUBJECTS: MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 41, series of 2015, dated July 27, 2015 from the
Sangguniang Bayan of Kitcharao, this province, “An Ordinance Creating the Office of the
Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA) Headed by a Senior Citizen in the Municipality of Kitcharao as
Mandated by R.A. 9257.”

ACTION TAKEN: The committee reviewed the subject municipal ordinance as to its propriety
and validity.

FINDINGS AND RECOMMENDATION:

Giving importance on the vital role of the elderly in nation-building, the Sangguniang
Bayan of Kitcharao enacted Municipal Ordinance No. 41, creating the Office of the Senior
Citizens Affairs (OSCA) in the municipality.

Cities and municipalities in the country are mandated to establish an OSCA office in
their respective localities as provided for Under Section 6 of R.A. 9994,”An Act Granting
Additional Benefits and Privileges to Senior Citizens, Further Amending Republic Act No. 7432,
As Amended, Otherwise Known As "An Act to Maximize the Contribution of Senior Citizens to
Nation Building, Grant Benefits and Special Privileges and for Other Purposes.”

The functions of the office, among others, are to plan, implement and monitor yearly
work programs in pursuance to the objectives of this ordinance; serve as a general
information and liaison center to serve the needs of senior citizens in the municipality of
Kitcharao; and be of assistance in filing complaints or charges against any establishment,
institution, or any agency refusing to comply with the privileges under R.A. 9994 before the
Department of Justice or any proper court of law. The office, under the office of the
Municipal Mayor, shall be headed by a senior citizen as provided for under Section 6 of said
ordinance.

Based on the intent of the ordinance, the committee finds the measure valid as
authorized under Section 447 (a)(5)(viv) of the Local Government Code of 1991 where the
sangguniang bayan as the legislative body of the municipality shall enact ordinances and
resolutions for the general welfare of the municipality and its inhabitants, and shall provide
for the care of the aged.

Considering the validity and noble purpose of said measure, the committee
recommends for its approval.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE 14™ SANGGUNIANG BAYAN OF KITCHARAO, AGUSAN DEL

SECTION 1. Title - This ordinance shall be known as the “ORDINANCE CREATING
THE OFFICE FOR THE SENIOR CITIZENS AFFAIRS (OSCA) IN THE

MUNICIPALITY OF KITCHARAO”,

SECTION 2. Legal Basis - This Ordinance is based on the provisions of Section. 12
and 23 of Republic Act No. 9257 entitled: “AN ACT GRANTING

ADDITIONAL BENEFITS AND PRIVILEGES TO SENIOR CITIZENS,
AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7432, OTHERWISE
KNOWN AS ,AN ACT TO MAXIMIZE THE CONTRIBUTION OF SENIOR
CITIZENS TO NATION BUILDING, GRANT BENEFITS AND SPECIAL
PRIVILEGES AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES”. /

SECTION 3. Purpose - As one of the basic services and facilities to be undertaken
by the municipal to establish mechanisms whereby the contribution of
the senior citizens are maximized; adopt measures whereby the senior
citizens are assisted and appreciated by the community as a whole,
establish a program beneficial to the senior citizens, their families and
the rest of the community that they serve; and establish community-
based health and rehabilitation programs in every political unit of

society.

SECTION 4. Definition of Terms - The terms for the purpose of this ordinance
shall mean:

a) “SENIOR CITIZEN” OR “ELDERLY” - shall mean any resident citizen
of the Philippines at least sixty (60) years old.

b) OSCA - shall refer to the Office for Senior Citizen Affairs headed bya
Senior Citizen.

SECTION 5. Authority and Functions of the Office of the Senior Citizens
Affairs (OSCA).

5.1 Authority - This ordinance is enacted to supplement provisions of
existing laws and ordinances in the municipality related
to the granting of benefits and privileges to Senior
Citizens.

5.2 Functions - The following are the functions of the Office for Senior
Citizens Affairs (OSCA).

5.2.1 To plan, implement and monitor yearly work programs in
pursuance to the objectives of this ordinance;

5.2.2 To draw up a list of available and required services which can
be provided by the Senior Citizens.

5.2.3 To maintain and regularly update on a quarterly basis, the list
of Senior Citizens and to issue, free of charge, which shall
be valid anywhere in the country;

5.2.4 To serve as a general information and liaison center to serve
the needs of the Senior Citizens;

5.2.5 To monitor compliance of the provisions of this ordinance and
other national laws, particularly the grant of special
discount and privileges to senior citizens;

5.2.6 To report to the Municipal Mayor, establishment found
violating any provision of this ordinance and applicable
national laws; and

5.2.7 To assist the Senior Citizens in filing complaints or charges
against any establishment, institution, or any agency
refusing to comply with the privileges under R.A. No. 9257
before the Department of Justice or any proper court of
law.

SECTION 6. The Office for Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA)- There is hereby
created under this ordinance the Office of the Senior Citizens Affairs
(OSCA) to be headed by a Senior Citizens who shall be appointed by
the Mayor for a term of three (3) years without reappointment from
a list of three (3) nominees of the Sangguniang Bayan. The old
MSWD Building located at barangay Songkoy, this municipality shall
be the official building for the head of the OSCA shall be assigned by
the Municipal Social Welfare and Development Officer.
