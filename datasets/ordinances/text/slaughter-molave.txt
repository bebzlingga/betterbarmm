                                 MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 12th-39-2026

AN ORDINANCE SUPPLEMENTING MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 11th-33-2023, OTHERWISE
KNOWN AS “AN ORDINANCE CREATING THE MUNICIPAL MEAT INSPECTION SERVICE (MMIS)
OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF MOLAVE, PROVINCE OF ZAMBOANGA DEL SUR,” BY PRESCRIBING
ADDITIONAL RULES AND REGULATIONS GOVERNING THE OPERATION OF THE MUNICIPAL
SLAUGHTERHOUSE, PROVIDING FOR TARIFF RATES AND BUTCHER’S FEES AND FOR OTHER
PURPOSES.

      WHEREAS, Section 16 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local
Government Code of 1991, provides that every local government unit shall exercise the
powers which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare;

       WHEREAS, the Municipality of Molave has previously enacted Municipal Ordinance
No. 11th-33-2023, creating the Municipal Meat Inspection Service (MMIS);

       WHEREAS, in order to ensure a comprehensive and effective governance of the
municipal slaughterhouse, there is a need to adopt additional rules and regulations to
enhance sanitary practices, food safety, and operational discipline;

       WHEREAS, the tariff rates and butcher’s fees are rationalized, reasonably related to
the cost of slaughterhouse operations, proportionate to the type and weight of animals
processed, and consistent with the regulatory purpose of the Municipal Revenue Code;

     NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Sangguniang Bayan of Molave,
Zamboanga del Sur, in session assembled that:

       SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as the “Supplemental
Slaughterhouse Regulatory and Tariff Ordinance of the Municipality of Molave”.

       SECTION 2. PURPOSE. This Ordinance establishes additional operational regulations
and prescribes authorized tariff rates and butcher’s fees for services rendered at the
Municipal Slaughterhouse, consistent with national meat safety standards and the
Municipal Revenue Code.

       SECTION 3. COVERAGE. This Ordinance shall apply to all butchers, assistants,
workers, meat dealers, transporters, and individuals or entities availing of services at the
Molave Municipal Slaughterhouse.

       SECTION 4. ADDITIONAL RULES AND REGULATIONS. The following additional rules
and regulations shall govern Molave Slaughterhouse operations:
   a. Butchers and their assistants shall practice the following:
       - Shall secure a Mayor’s Permit or License;
       - Shall wear proper attire –wear white boots while working.
       - Shall not be allowed to work when under the influence of alcohol.
       - Shall maintain cleanliness at all times.
       - Shall be liable to pay or replace any damages incurred to the MSLH facilities
          (tiles, faucets, boiling vat (kawa), etc.)
       - Shall observed Good Hygienic Slaughtering Practices when slaughtering food
          animals.
       - Shall be allowed to enter only from 12:01 AM, except on special occasions
          (e.g., fiestas, Christmas and other holidays), when entry shall begin at 9:00 PM.

     b. Meat Inspection Requirement – No meat shall leave the slaughterhouse without
        proper inspection by the Meat Inspector on duty.
     c. Meat Release Schedule from the Molave Slaughterhouse:
               Carabao/Cattle: 2:00 AM – 3:30 AM
               Pigs: 3:31 AM – 5:00 AM
               Others: 5:01 AM onwards

     d. Animal Entry Requirements. - In addition to the documentary requirements
        provided under Section 11 of Municipal Ordinance No. 11th-33-2023, a Barangay
        Certification shall be required for the entry of food animals, particularly pigs and
        goats, into the Municipal Slaughterhouse (MSLH). The Barangay Certification shall
        attest that the animals originated from a barangay are free from any reported
        cases of animal diseases and that the animals have undergone the necessary local
        monitoring prior to transport, in compliance with existing biosecurity and animal
        health regulations.
     e. Ban on Slaughtering Pregnant Large Animals – Pregnant cattle or carabaos are not
        allowed for slaughter.
     f. Emergency Slaughter –No animals shall proceed for slaughter until an inspector has
        carried out ante-mortem inspection. Exceptions may be under emergency
        slaughter procedure where a delay in carrying out ante-mortem inspection would
        result in undue suffering of the animal and with the Meat Inspector’s approval.
     g. Prohibition on Burning Rubber/Plastic – Using these to heat the boiling vat is strictly
        prohibited.
     h. No Littering – Throwing of garbage in or around the slaughterhouse is strictly
        prohibited.
     i. Vehicle Washing Ban – Washing of animal transport vehicles inside the
        slaughterhouse is prohibited.
     j. Vehicle Washing Exceptions – Only the official Meat Transport Vehicle may be
        washed inside.
     k. Slaughterhouse Closing Time – Operations close between 8:00 PM and 12:00 AM.

        SECTION 5. AUTHORIZED TARIFF RATES FOR QUALIFIED BUTCHERS. The following tariff
rates, exclusive of slaughter fees, shall be paid to duly licensed and qualified butchers for
butchering services at the Municipal Slaughterhouse:
            • Hogs: ₱5.00 per kilogram
            • Cattle/Cow: ₱1,000.00 per head
            • Carabao: ₱1,500.00 per head
            • Goat: ₱400.00 per head
            • Chicken: ₱40.00 per head
            • Duck: ₱60.00 per head
            • Turkey: ₱60.00 per head

Note: The following rates shall constitute the maximum allowable service fees that may be
charged by duly licensed and accredited butchers for butchering services rendered within
the Municipal Slaughterhouse. These fees shall be paid directly to the butcher and shall
not form part of municipal revenues.

        SECTION 6. BUTCHER’S FEE FOR LECHON (INCLUSIVE PACKAGE). The following fees
shall apply for lechon processing, inclusive of slaughter fee, butchering, spices (“lamas”),
and firewood (“sugnod”), unless otherwise specified:
             • Hogs (Lechon)
                   - ₱1,500.00 – 35 kilos and below
                   - ₱1,600.00 – 36 kilos to 50 kilos
                   - ₱1,700.00 – 51 kilos to 70 kilos
                   - ₱1,800.00 – 71 kilos and above
             • Pork Belly (Lechon Belly)
                   - ₱500.00 – 3 kilos and below
                   - ₱600.00 – 4 kilos to 5 kilos
                   - ₱700.00 – 6 kilos to 7 kilos
                   - ₱900.00 – 8 kilos and above
             • Cattle/Cow (Lechon).
                   - ₱5,000.00 per head (inclusive of slaughter fee; spices “lamas” and
                      firewood “sugnod” shall be provided by the owner)

        SECTION 7. REMITTANCE OF SLAUGHTER FEES. In all applicable cases, the butcher
shall remit the corresponding slaughter fee to the Municipal Treasurer or his/her duly
authorized representative in accordance with the Municipal Revenue Code. Failure to
remit shall subject the offender to penalties under existing ordinances.

        SECTION 8. PENALTIES. Any individual found violating any provision of this Ordinance
shall be subject to the following penalties, depending on the gravity and frequency of the
offense, to wit:
        - First Offense: Suspension of Mayor’s Permit or License for a period not
            exceeding thirty (30) days.
        - Second Offense: Cancellation of License or Permit, and permanent
            disqualification from working in the municipal slaughterhouse.

          SECTION 9. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If any provision of this Ordinance is declared
unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions shall not be affected and shall remain
in full force and effect.

        SECTION 10. REPEALING CLAUSE. All ordinances, rules, and regulations inconsistent
with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

       SECTION 11. EFFECTIVITY. This ordinance shall take effect after compliance with the
posting requirements in accordance with Republic Act No. 7160.

          ENACTED, this 15th day of April, 2026 at Molave, Zamboanga del Sur.
