AN ORDINANCE GRANTING HAZARD AND SUBSISTENCE ALLOWANCES, AND A
ONE-TIME GRATUITY PAY TO REGISTERED AND/OR ACCREDITED BARANGAY
HEALTH WORKERS (BHWs), APPOINTED BARANGAY NUTRITION SCHOLARS
(BNSs), AND APPOINTED BARANGAY SANITARY INSPECTORS (BSIs) IN THE
MUNICIPALITY OF BRAULIO E. DUJALI, DAVAO DEL NORTE

WHEREAS, Republic Act No. 7883, otherwise known as the “Barangay Health Workers’
Benefits and Incentives Act of 1995,” recognizes the invaluable services rendered by
Barangay Health Workers (BHWs) in the delivery of primary health care services and
mandates the provision of benefits and incentives;

WHEREAS, Barangay Nutrition Scholars (BNSs) play a crucial role in delivering
nutrition services and other related activities in the barangay and are recognized for
their volunteer work and contribution to community health;

WHEREAS, Barangay Sanitary Inspectors (BSIs) are essential in promoting public
health and sanitation at the barangay level;

WHEREAS, the Local Government Unit of Braulio E. Dujali recognizes the dedicated
service and vital contributions of BHWs, BNSs, and BSIs to the health and well-being
of its constituents, often in challenging and at-risk environments;

WHEREAS, it is the policy of the Local Government Unit to provide adequate support
and incentives to these front-line health and sanitation workers; KE

SECTION 1. TITLE

This Ordinance shall be known as the “BHW, BNS, and BSI Benefits and Gratuity
Ordinance of Braulio E. Dujali.

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY

It is hereby declared the policy of the Local Government Unit of Braulio E. Dujali to
acknowledge, protect, and support the invaluable services rendered by Barangay
Health Workers, Barangay Nutrition Scholars, and Barangay Sanitary Inspectors by
providing them with hazard and subsistence allowances, and a one-time gratuity
pay, in recognition of their continuous and dedicated service.

SECTION 3: COVERAGE

This Ordinance shall cover all duly registered and/or accredited Barangay Health
Workers (BHWs), officially appointed Barangay Nutrition Scholars (BNSs) and
Barangay Sanitary Inspectors (BSIs), as certified by their respective Punong
Barangays, who are actively rendering service within the territorial jurisdiction of
the Municipality of Braulio E. Dujali, Davao del Norte.

SECTION 4. DEFINITION OF TERMS
For this Ordinance, the following terms shall be defined as follows:

a. Barangay Health Worker (BHW) - A person who has undergone training
programs under any accredited government or non-government organization
and who voluntarily renders primary health care services in the community.

b. Barangay Nutrition Scholar (BNS) - A barangay-based volunteer worker
responsible for delivering nutrition services and other related activities,
officially appointed by the Barangay.

c. Barangay Sanitary Inspector (BSI) - A barangay-based worker responsible for
monitoring and promoting environmental sanitation and hygiene, officially
appointed by the Barangay

d. Registered - A designation granted by the Municipal Health Office to personnel
who have fulfilled the required service duration, passed competency
assessments, and demonstrated satisfactory performance.

e. Accredited - A status conferred upon personnel who, after consistent service
and field performance, are endorsed by the Municipal Health Office and
officially recorded in the registry of the Local Health Board and the Provincial
Health Office.

f. Appointed - Refers to individuals formally designated by the barangay and
endorsed by the Municipal Health Office to perform active roles in health,
nutrition, or sanitation services.

g. Rehired Personnel - Formerly contracted BHWs, BNSs, or BSIs who have
completed a term of service and have been officially reappointed.

h. Hazard Allowance - Compensation for BHWs, BNSs, and BSIs exposed to
situations, conditions, or factors in the work environment where foreseeable
but unavoidable danger or risks exist, which may adversely endanger their
health or life.

i. Subsistence Allowance - Compensation granted for BHWs, BNSs, and BSIs who
render service within the premises of isolated barangay health stations or
other designated areas, making their services available at any and all times.

j. Gratuity Pay - A one-time financial recognition granted to eligible personnel
who have rendered a minimum of five (5) continuous years of satisfacto

SECTION 5: HAZARD ALLOWANCE

All registered and/or accredited Barangay Health Workers (BHWs), and officially
appointed Barangay Nutrition Scholars (BNSs) and Barangay Sanitary Inspectors
(BSIs), as certified by the respective Punong Barangays and formally endorsed by the
Municipal Health Board, shall be granted a hazard allowance of not less than One
Thousand Pesos (?1,000.00) per month, payable quarterly.

The determination and validation of exposure to risks shall be made by the Local
Health Board, consistent with the provisions of Republic Act No. 7883.

SECTION 6: SUBSISTENCE ALLOWANCE

All registered and/or accredited BHWs, and officially appointed BNSs and BSlIs, as
certified by the respective Punong Barangays and formally endorsed by the
Municipal Health Board, who render service within the premises of isolated barangay
health stations or other designated areas in the performance of their duties, shall be
entitled to a subsistence allowance of Fifty Pesos (P50.00) per day.

This allowance shall be computed in accordance with prevailing circumstances, as
determined by the Local Government Unit.

SECTION 7: ONE-TIME GRATUITY PAY

In recognition of the continuous and dedicated service rendered by Barangay Health
Workers (BHWs), Barangay Nutrition Scholars (BNSs), and Barangay Sanitary
Inspectors (BSIs), a one-time gratuity pay shall be granted to eligible and qualified
personnel, subject to the following conditions:

a. A one-time gratuity pay in the amount of Five Thousand Pesos
(P5,000.00) shall be granted to personnel who have rendered at least
five (5) continuous years of service in their respective barangay
assignments.

b. An additional gratuity pay of One Thousand Pesos (?1,000.00) shall be
granted for every year of continuous service rendered beyond the initial
five (5) years, computed cumulatively.

c. Rehired personnel who have previously received a lump sum gratuity
pay shall be eligible for another gratuity pay upon rendering a new
period of five (5) continuous years of service, and for each year
thereafter, in accordance with subsections (a) and (b), provided that the
personnel has not yet reached the age of sixty (60) years.

d. The grant of the gratuity pay shall be subject to the availability of funds
and the submission of verified service records, as certified by the
Punong Barangay and attested by the Municipal Health Officer or other
designated authority.

SECTION 9. FUNDING

The necessary funds for the implementation of this Ordinance shall be sourced as
follows:

a. Fifty percent (50%) of the Hazard Allowance and the entire Fifty Pesos (P50.00)
per day Subsistence Allowance shall be appropriated from the General Fund of
the Local Government Unit of Braulio E. Dujali.

b. The remaining fifty percent (50%) of the Hazard Allowance shall be sourced
from the Trust Fund - PhilHealth’s Konsulta Package Benefit fund. Utilization
of this fund shall be subject to the applicable rules, regulations, and issuances
of the Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth), and only upon
clear determination that such use is permissible and consistent with the fund’s
purpose.

c. The Gratuity Pay shall be appropriated solely from the General Fund of the
Local Government Unit of Braulio E. Dujali.

SECTION 10. IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS (IRR)

Within sixty (60) days from the approval of this Ordinance, the Municipal Health
Office shall formulate and issue the necessary Implementing Rules and Regulations
(IRR) to ensure the proper and effective implementation of this Ordinance.

SECTION 11. MONITORING AND EVALUATION

The Local Health Board (LHB) shall regularly monitor and evaluate the
implementation of this Ordinance, including the proper disbursement of allowances
and gratuity pay, and the continued active service of the beneficiaries.

SECTION 12. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE

If any provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the
remaining provisions not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and
effect.
