                         EXPLANATORY NOTE

        Delay in any transaction with the government hinders the
country’s quest for speedy economic recovery. Fast financial growth is
vital for the acceleration of local and national wealth which is beneficial
to both the citizenry and government sector.

        Employment of dilatory tactics by not acting with dispatch the
client’s application for business permit with the local government unit
maybe categorized as red tape. This attitude oftentimes results to
under the table display of irregularity. This is purely anomalous and
must therefore be totally eradicated outright.

      Instead of doing dishonest behavior, the personnel concerned
should fast track the processing time in facilitating the Business Permit

otherwise known as the Anti-Red Tape Act (ARTA) of 2007.

       Relative to the herein above premises, it is believed that the
crafting of an ordinance to shorten the time in processing documentary
requirements for business permit deserves consideration by the August
Body.

                        ORDINANCE NO. 16
                          Series of 2015

  “AN ORDINANCE SHORTENING THE PROCESSING TIME IN
GRANTING MUNICIPAL BUSINESS PERMITS AND/OR LICENSES
 TO CLIENTS/APPLICANTS CONSISTENT WITH REPUBLIC ACT
NO. 9485, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS “ANTI-RED TAPE ACT (ARTA)
                       OF 2007.”

      BE IT ORDAINED by the Sangguniang Bayan of Catarman,
      Camiguin, in regular session assembled:

      Section 1. TITLE – This measure shall be known and referred to
                 as AN ORDINANCE STREAMLINING THE
                 PROCESSING TIME OF ALL APPLICATIONS FOR
                 MUNICIPAL      BUSINESS      PERMITS      AND/OR
                 LICENSES.

      Section 2. STATEMENT OF POLICIES

                  2.1 The Local Government Unit recognizes the
                      importance of improving its potential growth
                      through enhancing its competitiveness at the
                      local levels. This can only be achieved through
                      reforms that reduce the cost of doing business in
                      the locality and address other policy issues that
                      discourage investors.

                  2.2 Republic Act No. 9485 mandates all government
                      instrumentalities to provide efficient delivery of
                      services to the public by reducing bureaucratic
                      red tape and preventing graft and corruption and
                      providing penalties thereof. The ARTA which
                      provides the legal basis for re-engineering
                      current business processing system at the local
                      level sets benchmark for processing simple and
                      complex transactions which should be applied to
                      BPLS nationwide.

   Section 4. FIVE STANDARD STEPS IN APPLYING FOR NEW
               OR RENEWAL OF BUSINESS PERMITS

               4.1 Securing application form from the municipality;
               4.2 Filing/submission of the accomplished
                   application from with attached documentary
                   requirements;
               4.3 One-time assessment of taxes, fees and
                   charges;
               4.4 One-time payment of taxes, fees and charges;
               4.5 Securing the Mayor’s Permit upon submission of
                   Official Receipts as proof of payment of taxes,
                   fees and charges imposed by the LGU;

   Section 5. CLEARANCE FROM BARANGAY – No license or
              business permit may be issued by the LGU
              (municipality) unless a clearance is first obtained
              from the barangay where such business is located or
              conducted.

                The Sangguniang Barangay concerned may impose
                a reasonable fee in issuing a clearance. This
                clearance shall be acted upon within seven (7)
                working days from filing thereof.

   Section 6. JOINT INSPECTION TEAM – a composite team
             whose members come from various LGU agencies
             implementing business related regulations shall be
             organized to conduct joint inspection of business
             enterprise instead of individual inspections.       All
             members of the team shall affix their signature at the
             space provided for in a separate Joint Certificate of
             Compliance relative to applicant’s application for
             business permit/license.

   Section 7.     COMPOSITION OF THE JOINT INSPECTION
                TEAM – The Team is composed of the following:

                7.1 Business Permit/Licensing Officer
                7.2 The Municipal Engineer
                7.3 The Municipal Health Officer/Representative
                7.4 The Municipal Planning and Development
                Coordinator or
                        Zoning/Environment Officer
                7.5 The Municipal Treasurer
                7.6 The Municipal Fire Marshal

   Section 8. ONE STOP SHOP – All LGU personnel who are
              involved in processing business permit applications
              or Business Permit Licensing System (BPLS) shall
              sit-down in a single common site or location
              designated for all concerned agencies to receive and
              process application for business registration through
              a streamlined procedure.

   Section 9. PROCESSING TIME – The processing time allotted
              to a new application for business permit shall not
              take more than ten (10) days. However, the LGU
                concerned are enjoined to strive for five (5) days or less,
                an average processing day with streamlined BPLS. For
                the renewal of business permit, the processing time shall
                not take more than five (5) days but the concerned LGU
                are like enjoined to strive for one (1) day or less.

   Section 10. SIGNATORIES IN BUSINESS PERMIT - In the Municipal
               Business Permit and/or License only two (2) signatories
               are required, the Municipal Mayor and the Municipal
               Treasurer of their authorized/deputized representatives.

   Section 11. RELEASE OF BUSINESS PERMIT – Upon presentation
               of official receipts evidencing payment of taxes, fees
               and/or charges, the Municipal Business Permit/License
               maybe released by the Municipal Mayor after affixing
               signatures on the document by the required signatories,

       Section 12. PENALTY CLAUSE – Any person found violating any
                  prohibitory provision of this ordinance shall be penalized
                  by a fine of Five Hundred (P500.00) Pesos or an
                  imprisonment of one (1) week or both fine and
                  imprisonment at the discretion of the court.

       Section 13. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE – Should any provision of this
                   ordinance or parts hereof be declared unconstitutional or
                   invalid, the other provision separate from the flaws shall
                   remain effective and in full force.

       Section 14. REPEALING CLAUSE – All ordinances, issuances, local
                   laws or executive orders inconsistent with the provisions
                   of this ordinance are hereby repealed or amended
                   accordingly.

       Section 15. EFFECTIVITY – This ordinance shall take effect January
                   1, 2016 and upon approval and after complying
                   publication and/or posting requirements as provided for
                   under Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the
                   Local Government Code of 1991.
