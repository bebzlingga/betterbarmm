Municipal Ordinance No. 2026-09
Series of 2026

AN ORDINANCE REGULATING THE KEEPING OF DOGS AND OTHER ANIMALS AND PROHIBITING
ANIMALS FROM CAUSING HARM, INJURY, OR DISTURBANCE TO PEOPLE, OTHER ANIMALS, OR
RESIDENTS WITHIN THE MUNICIPALITY OF MARIBOJOC, BOHOL.

Authored by: Hon. Jedfrey S. Veloso

WHEREAS, Section 16 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code
of 1991, provides that every local government unit shall exercise the powers necessary for the promotion of
the general welfare, particularly to promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced
ecology, and maintain peace and order and the comfort and convenience of their inhabitants;

WHEREAS, Section 444(b)(1)(ii) of the same Code empowers the Municipal Mayor to enforce all

laws and ordinances relative to the governance of the municipality and to ensure the delivery of basic services
including public safety and environmental management;

WHEREAS, Section 447(a)(1)(vi) authorizes the Sangguniang Bayan to enact ordinances to protect
the environment and the health and safety of the populace;

WHEREAS, Republic Act No. 8485, otherwise known as the Animal Welfare Act of 1998 (as
amended by RA 10631), and Republic Act No. 9482, otherwise known as the Anti-Rabies Act of 2007,

mandate the proper care, management, and control of dogs, as well as the promotion of responsible pet
ownershin:

WHEREAS, uncontrolled, neglected, or improperly managed animals — including dogs, livestock,
poultry, and other domesticated or captive animals — have caused injuries, nuisances, property damage, and
public disturbances within the Municipality of Maribojoc.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Sangguniang Bayan of Maribojoc, in session duly
assembled, that:

Section 1. Title. This Ordinance shall be known as the “Responsible Animal Ownership and Control
Ordinance of the Municipality of Maribojoc.”

Section 2. Declaration of Policy. tis hereby declared the policy of the Municipality of Maribojoc to promote
responsible animal ownership, ensure public safety, protect animal welfare, prevent animal-related
disturbances and injuries, and foster peaceful coexistence between residents, animals, and the community.

Section 3. Definition of Terms. For the purpose of this Ordinance, the following terms shall mean:

a) Animal - Any domesticated, captive, or tamed animal, including but not limited to dogs, cats,

livestock, poultry, and other pets kept by a person for companionship, security, livelihood, or other
ses.

b) Dleier — Any person who owns, keeps, harbors, controls, or has custody of an animal.

c) Stray Animal - Any animal found roaming, wandering, or unattended in public or private places
without proper supervision or control by its owner. a .

d) Impoundment - The act of capturing, confining, and caring for an animal found in violation of this
Ordinance by authorized municipal personnel.

e) Disturbance — Any act or behavior of an animal that causes nuisance or danger, including excessive
noise, aggression, chasing, attacking persons or other animals, or damaging property.

f) Public Place - Any street, road, park, school, market, or other place accessible to the public.

Section 4. Prohibited Acts. It shall be unlawful for any person to:

a) Allow any animal to roam freely in public places or on private property without the consent of the
property owner;

b) Allow any animal to attack, bite, chase, injure, or threaten any person or other animal;

c) Fail to properly confine, leash, restrain, cage, or control an animal appropriate to its species and size;

d) Fail to vaccinate dogs against rabies and other required diseases in accordance with existing national
laws;

e) Abandon, neglect, or maltreat any animal in violation of animal welfare laws:

f} Refuse responsibility or compensation for injury, death, or damage caused by an animal under one's
ownership or control:

g) Keep animals in a manner that poses danger to public health, safety, sanitation, or peace and order.

Section 5. Responsibilities of Animal Owners. All animal owners within the Municipality shall:
a) Ensure proper care, humane treatment, adequate food, potable water, shelter, and sanitation for
their animals;
b) Properly confine, restrain, or control animals at all times to prevent escape or harm to the public;

c) Register animals subject to registration requirements, particularly dogs, with the Municipal
Agriculture or Veterinary Office;

d) we i timely vaccination of dogs against rabies and compliance with other applicable animal health
regulations;

e) Immediately report to the proper authorities any incident involving animal bites, attacks, or injuries;
f) Clean and dispose of animal waste properly, especially in public places.

Ordinance shall be subject to the following penalties, without prejudice to civil liability for damages:

Higher penalties are imposed for livestock due to the greater risk to public safety, traffic, sanitation,
and property damage.

C. AGGRAVATING CIRCUMSTANCE - An additional fine of One Thousand Pesos (P1,000.00) shall
be imposed if the violation involves any of the following:

e Injury or death of a person or another animal;

e Damage to crops, fences, or private or public property;

e Obstruction of roads or involvement in vehicular accidents;
e Repeated violations within the same calendar year.

D. IMPOUNDMENT AND FEES — Impounded animals shall be released only upon:
e Payment of the applicable fine;
e Payment of impounding and daily maintenance fees as determined by the Municipal Agriculture
Office; and
e Compliance with registration, vaccination, or confinement requirements.

Failure to comply may result in continued impoundment, adoption, sale, or humane disposition of the
animal, in accordance with existing animal welfare and public health laws.

E. BARANGAY SHARE IN COLLECTED FINES - All fines collected under this Ordinance shall be
distributed as follows:
e Sixty percent (60%) — Municipality (General Fund)
e Forty percent (40%) — Barangay where the violation occurred

The barangay share shall be used exclusively for:

Animal control and monitoring activities;

Support to Barangay Livestock Aide (BALA) operations;
Public information and education campaigns;

Purchase of basic animal control or sanitation supplies.

Remittance, utilization, and accounting of funds shall be subject to existing treasury, budgeting, and
Commission on Audit (COA) rules and regulations.

BARANGAY-MUNICIPAL REMITTANCE AND SEMI-ANNUAL CONSOLIDATION
The barangay share in collected fines shall be released and remitted in accordance with the following
guidelines:
1. All fines collected under this Ordinance shall be officially receipted and recorded by the Municipal
Treasurer's Office.
2. The barangay share of collected fines shall be remitted on a semi-annual basis, covering the
following periods:
a) First Semester: January to June
b) Second Semester: July to December
3. The Municipality shall prepare a consolidated semi-annual statement of collections per barangay
and remit the corresponding barangay share within fifteen (15) working days after the end of
each semester.
4. Each semi-annual remittance shall be supported by a Statement of Collection, indicating the
following:

5. The concerned barangay shall issue an acknowledgment receipt upon remittance and shall

FINAL LIABILITY CLAUSE
In addition to the foregoing penalties, the owner shall be liable for medical expenses, veterinary costs,

repairs, or compensation for any injury, death, or damage caused by the animal.

Section 7. Impoundment and Disposition of Stray or Uncontrolled Animals. Stray or uncontrolled
animals shall be captured and impounded by the Municipal Agriculture Office or other authorized personnel.

Owners may reclaim impounded animals upon payment of impounding and maintenance fees and
compliance with registration and vaccination requirements, when applicable.

Unclaimed animals after three (3) days may be subject to adoption, transfer, or humane disposition in
accordance with RA 8485, RA 9482, and other applicable laws.

Section 8. Implementation and Enforcement. This Ordinance shall be implemented by the Municipal
Agriculture Office, in coordination with the Barangays, Municipal Health Office, Philippine National Police,
and Barangay Livestock Aide (BALA).

The Barangay Livestock Aide personnel, under the technical supervision of the Municipal Agriculture Office,
shall assist in the monitoring, inspection, reporting, and initial response to violations involving animals within
their respective barangays.

Barangay Officials and Barangay Tanods shall likewise assist in enforcement, apprehension, and
maintenance of peace and order in relation to the implementation of this Ordinance.

Section 9. Information and Education Campaign. The Municipal Government, through the Municipal
Agriculture Office, shall conduct regular information drives and seminars on responsible animal ownership,
animal welfare, and rabies prevention, in coordination with concerned national agencies,

Section 10. Funding. Funds necessary for the implementation of this Ordinance shall be included in the
annual budget of the Municipality under the concerned offices.

Section 11, Separability Clause. If any part or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or
invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

Section 12. Repealing Clause. All ordinances, executive orders, rules, and regulations inconsistent with
this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

Section 13. Effectivity. This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

Enacted by the Sangguniang Bayan of Maribojoc, Bohol on the 27th day of January 2026.
