                                    ORDINANCE NO.02, SERIES OF 2024

"AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITING THE ENTRY AND OCCUPATION OF FOREST LANDS WITHIN
MUNICIPALITY OF CARRASCAL, SURIGAO DEL SUR AND IMPOSING APPROPRIATE
PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS THEREOF."



                                          INTRODUCED BY:
                                HONORABLE LEO VI NO M. CONSTANTINO



       "WHEREAS, it has been observed that forest lands especially along the national
road within Barangays Adlay and Bon-ot have been occupied by informal settlers or illegal
occupants;

         SECTION 3. STATEMENT OF PURPOSE.


         1. To prevent the cutting of trees and other vegetations within and nearby areas.
         2. To prevent the areas from being polluted and degraded. According to CENRO
            Personnel, illegal occupants have no proper disposal of wastes.
         3. To make the scenery along the road, covered with lush vegetation thereby
            making it pleasurable to the passersby.


         SECTION 4. MANNERS OF IMPLEMENTATION. As additional function of the Office
of the MENRO, he shall have periodic inspection and monitoring of forest lands specially
the national road of Barangay Bon-ot and Adlay to determine if there are illegal occupants.
In his monitoring activity, he shall coordinate with concerned barangay officials. He shall
also seek assistance from the CENRO Cantilan and PNP. Further, He shall install "DO NOT
ENTER" signs in strategic location.

       As a support to the prevention of illegal occupation of forest lands, Local
Government Unit of Carrascal shall require mining companies that their trucks/equipment
and those of their hauling contractor should have their motor pool or parking areas within
their MPSA and not on forest lands specially along national road.

         SECTION 5. PENALTIES.


         a.) For 1st Offense - reprimand and a fine of P 1,000.00.

         b.) For 2 n ' Offense - fine of P 2,000.00

         c.) For 3 rl Offence - fine of P 2,500.00


      SECTION 6. APPROPRIATIONS. The Local Government Unit of Carrascal, Surigao del
Sur shall appropriate funds for this ordinance.


         SECTION 7. REPEALING CLAUSE. All existing Ordinance inconsistent hereof are
hereby repealed or modified accordingly.


       SECTION 8. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If any part or provision of this Ordinance is
declared unconstitutional or invalid by competent court, other parts or provisions hereof
which are not affected shall continue in full force an effect.


         SECTION 9. APPLICABILITY. This Ordinance shall apply to the structures and other
establishments or enacted prior to the approval of the ordinance.

         SECTION 10. EFFECTIVITY. SECTION 13. EFFECTIVITY. This Ordinance shall take
effect after ten (io) days from the date a copy thereof is posted in the bulletin board at
the entrance of the Municipal Hall and in at least two (2) other conspicuous places in the
local government unit.

       "WHEREAS, according to CENRO Cantilan personnel there are no less than thirty
two (32) families residing within said portion of forest lands engaged in selling of gasoline
and other commodities;

         "WHEREAS, the DENR is the primary government agency responsible for the
conservation, management, development and proper use of country's environment and
natural resources; and

      "WHEREAS, under Section 447 of RA 7160, particularly (i) (vi) the Sangguniang
Bayan could enact ordinances to protect the environment and impose penalties for acts
which result to the pollution and degradation of forest lands and finally of ecological
imbalance.

                                             " NOW THEREFORE "

         "BE IT ORDAINED by the Sangguniang Bayan of the Municipality of Carrascal,
Surigao del Sur, in session duly assembled that:

       SECTION 1. TITLE. This ordinance shall be known as "An Ordinance Prohibiting the
Entry and Occupation of Forest Lands within the Municipality of Carrascal, Surigao del Sur
and Imposing Appropriate Penalties for Violation of Thereof.

         SECTION 2. DEFINITION OF TERMS.

         1. Public Forest - is the mass of lands of the public domain which has not been the
             subject of the present system of classification for the determination of which
             lands are needed for forest purposes and which are not.
         2. Permanent Forest of Forest Reserves - refer to those lands of the public
             domain which have been the subject of the present system of classification and
             declared as needed for forest purposes.
         3.  Forest  Reservation - refers to forest lands which have been reserved by the
             President of the Philippines for any specific purpose.
         4. Forest Lands includes the public forest, the permanent forest orforest reserves
             and forest reservations.
         5.  Alienable  and Disposable Lands - refer to those lands of the public domain
             which have been the subject of the present system of classification and
             declared as not needed for forest purposes.
         6. Lease - is privilege granted by the DENR to a person to occupy and possess, in
             consideration of specified rentals, any forest land in order to undertake any
             authorized activity therein.
         7. Permit - is a short term privilege granted by the DENR to a person to utilize any
             limited forest land without any right of occupation and possession therein.
         8. Illegal Occupants - anybody who enters and occupies forest land without lease
             or permit issued by the DENR.
         9.  DENR   - Department of Environment and Natural Resources.
         10. MENRO - Municipal Environment and natural Resources.
         ii. Occupation - is the act of possessing and holding of land, inhabit or dwell
              therein for any purpose.
