THE TRANSPORTATION AND TRAFFIC CODE OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF LOON, BOHOL



                                           ARTICLE I
                                 SHORT TITLE AND SCOPE

Section 1. Title of Code. This Code shall be known as the Transportation and Traffic Code of
the Municipality of Loon, Bohol.

Section 2. Scope of Code. The provisions of this Code shall administer and control, as far as
they apply, the operation of all motor vehicles in the Municipality of Loon.

Section 3. Application. This Code shall apply to all kinds of vehicles for public or private use,
which avails any of the roads, public or private, within the territorial jurisdiction of Loon.



                                          ARTICLE II
                                   DEFINITION OF TERMS

Section 4. Words and Phrases Defined.          As used in this Code, the following terms are
defined:

   a. Alleys – refer to a narrow street, passageway or by way.

   b. Automobiles – a usually four-wheeled automotive vehicle designed for passenger
      transportation or private services.

   c. Center Line – shall mean continuous or broken line marked upon the surface of roadway
      by point or by other traffic control devices to indicate such portions of the roadway
      allocated to traffic proceeding in the two opposite directions.

   d. Driver – shall mean any person duly licensed to mechanically operate a motor vehicle.
   e. Garage – shall mean any building in which two or more motor vehicles, either with or
      without drivers, are kept ready for hire to the public, but shall not include street stands,

        public services stations, or other public places designated by proper authority as parking
        spaces for motor vehicles for hire while awaiting or soliciting business.


   f.   Highways – shall mean every public thoroughfare, public boulevard, driveway, avenue,
        park alley and callejon, but shall not include roadway upon grounds owned by private
        persons, colleges, or other similar institutions.

   g. Hitching - passenger who rides on the outside of any vehicle.

   h. Lanes – refer to a narrow pathway or by way.

   i.   Marked Crosswalk – shall mean any portion of a roadway at an intersection or elsewhere
        distinctly indicated for the pedestrians crossing by lines or other art marking on the
        surface.

   j.   Motorcycle for rent – shall mean a motorcycle offered for rent or for use in consideration
        of a sum of money, commonly known as “habal-habal”. However, in all instances, the
        motorcycle shall be driven by a duly licensed driver.

   k. Motorized Tricycle Operator’s Permit (MTOP) – is the document issued by the
      Sangguniang Bayan granting franchise or license to operate issued to a person, natural
      or juridical, allowing him/it to operate motorized tricycle over zones specified therein
      pursuant to Section 447, 3vi, RA 7160.

   l.   Motor Vehicle – shall mean any vehicle propelled by any power other than muscular
        power using the public highways.

   m. Motor Scooter – shall mean any motorcycle propelled by a motor which produces not
      more than (five) 5 horse power.

   n. Overloading – an excessive load of passengers or the number of passengers on any
      vehicle which is more than its maximum capacity.

   o. Over speeding – too much speed or rapidity on moving upon travelling, in violation of the
      speed-limit rules, or reckless driving.

   p. Owner – shall mean the actual legal owner of a motor vehicle, in whose name such
      vehicle is duly registered with the Land Transportation Commission. The ‘’owner’’ of a
      government-owned motor vehicle is the LGU, the responsibility of which is assigned to
      the head of the office/agency to which the said motor vehicle belongs.

   q. Parking Area – refers to a designated area reserved for parking of classified vehicles for
      public or private use, without limit as to its specific time of parking, unless otherwise
      agreed upon by the Association to which the vehicle-operators are associated, or by
      virtue of an Executive Order, or any provision of an ordinance appurtenant thereto.

   r.   Parking or parked – for purposes of this Ordinance, shall mean to include a motor
        vehicle which is brought to a stop on any side of a street, not prohibited by any rule or
        regulation, with blinking or hazard lights on but without a driver or left unattended,
        provided it does not exceed 5 minutes.

   s. Passenger Vehicle – shall mean the buses, jeepneys, multi-cabs, V-hire or four to eight
      (4-8) wheeler vehicles intended to transport passengers with or without cargoes.

   t.   Pedestrian – means any person who is required to use the marked crosswalk when
        crossing the highway, avenue and city street or walking on the sidewalk on the side by
        the Highway.

   u. Professional Driver – shall mean any driver paid for driving or operating a motor vehicle,
      whether for private or public use, or an owner of his own private vehicle, who has an

   v. Public Road – includes national, provincial, municipal or barangay roads, maintained at

   w. Private Road – refers to privately constructed roads at the expense of private funds.

       spaces inside vehicle are fully occupied by passengers /or cargoes.

   cc. Traffic – refers to any use of a street for purposes of travel, enjoyed by pedestrians, or
       passengers of any public or private vehicles, streets cars and other conveyances, or any
       ridden or herded animals, that affect public safety and convenience.

   dd. Traffic Control Devices – shall mean all signs, signals, pavement markings, traffic cones
       placed or erected by authority of the Municipal Traffic and Management Office (MTMO)
       or officials having jurisdiction for the purpose of regulating warning or guiding traffic.

   ee. Traffic Control Signals – shall mean any device manually or electronically operated by
       which traffic is alternatively directed to stop and to proceed or is otherwise controlled.

   ff. Traffic Violator – shall mean any person who violates any of the provisions of this
       Transportation and Traffic Code, or any traffic and transportation ordinances, rules,
       regulations, Executive Orders, circulars, or laws.

   gg. Trailers – shall mean a thing having any number of wheels when propelled or intended to
       be propelled by attachment to a motor vehicle, classified as separate motor vehicle with
       no power rating.

   hh. Tricycle – shall mean a motorcycle with attached cab intended to transport passengers
       or cargoes.

   ii. Vehicle – a machine, usually with wheels and an engine, used for transporting persons
       or goods; something used on land or roads for public transport or private services.

   jj. Zone – an area under special restrictions as to the type of buildings that may be erected.

Section 6. Municipal Traffic Management Board (MTMB)

               1.   Chairman - SB Chairman, Committee on Transportation

               3.   Chief, PNP Traffic Division - member
               4.   Private Sector Consultant - member
               5.   One (1) Representative from the Municipal Mayor who is a regular employee
               6.   MUTRA Head

       The private sector consultant and Mayor’s representative shall be appointed/designated
   by the Municipal Mayor.

  c) Functions and Duties of the Municipal Traffic Management Board.

         1. Conducts periodic review in the implementation of the Municipal Traffic Code and
            ordinances as well as the reports and the recommendations submitted by the
            Municipal Traffic Management Office;

         2. Evaluates the effectiveness of the traffic management schemes being
            implemented by the Municipal Government;

         3. Reviews the efficiency and effectiveness in the implementation of the Municipal
            Traffic Code and related ordinances and other rules and regulations by the
            Municipal Traffic Management Office; and

         4. Serves as a consultative and advisory body to the Municipal Mayor and the
            Sangguniang Bayan Committee on Transportation in matters pertaining to traffic
            operations and management in the Municipality of Loon, Bohol.

Section 8. The Municipal PNP Traffic Enforcers of the PNP Traffic Division and other
            Members of the Police Department

    a) The Municipal PNP traffic enforcers of the PNP Traffic Division are hereby given the
       authority and the primary responsibility and duty to enforce the provisions of this Code
       and all the national vehicle laws applicable to street traffic in the Municipality of Loon.

    b) Officers and members of the police department or such officers as are assigned by the
       Chief of Police are hereby authorized to direct all traffic by voice, hand or signal in
       conformance with the provisions of this Code; Provided, however, in the event of fire or
       other emergency or to expedite traffic or to safeguard pedestrians, officers of the police
       department may direct traffic as conditions may require not withstanding the provisions
       herein.

    c) Officers and members of the police department are hereby authorized to display
       portable signs within certain intersection forbidding vehicle drivers to perform specific
       maneuvers. Said portable signs shall only be displayed at a specified intersection
       during times of traffic congestion when in the opinion of the Chief of Police such
       restrictions are necessary to expedite the movement of the traffic through the
       intersection.

    d) Officers and members of the fire department when at the fire scene may direct or assist
       the police in directing traffic thereat or in the immediate vicinity.

                                  ARTICLE IV
                 OPERATION OF MOTOR VEHICLES AND TRAFFIC RULES

Section 10. Driver’s License
   a) No person shall operate any motor vehicle without having in his possession a valid
      license to drive a motor vehicle.

       The license shall be carried by the driver at all times when operating a motor vehicle,
    and shall be shown and/or surrendered for cause and upon demand to any person with
    authority to confiscate the same.

   b) No driver shall be allowed to drive with expired driver’s license.

   c) No driver on “Student Permit (SP)” privilege shall drive without being accompanied by a
      professional driver.

   d) No driver shall be allowed to carry or use any fake driver’s license.
   e) No foreigner driver shall be allowed to drive without carrying his “international driver’s
      license” which is valid for 100 days.

Section 11. Failure to carry Certificate of Registration and Official Receipt

   No driver is allowed to drive without carrying the Certificate of Registration and Official
Receipt.

Section 12. Wearing of Helmet

    No motorcycle driver or any motorcycle back-rider shall ride on a motorcycle on a national,
provincial, municipal, or barangay road, regardless of distance, without wearing of a standard
protective motorcycle helmet.

Section 13. Use of Improvised Muffler

   No vehicle with improvised muffler that produces loud and disturbing sound is allowed to
pass in any street of this municipality.

Section 14. Headlights
   a) Every motor vehicle of more than one meter of projected width “except tricycle”, while in
      use on any public highway shall bear two headlights, one on each side, with white or
      yellowish light visible from the front, which, not later than one-half hour after sunset and
      until at least one-half hour before sunrise and whenever weather conditions so require,
      shall both be lighted.

   b) Additional lamps and light may be carried, but no red lights shall be installed in front part
      of any public or private vehicle

   c) All motor vehicles shall be equipped with devices for varying the intensity of light, and
      the driver must dim the headings or tilt the beams downward whenever the vehicle is
      being operated on well lighted streets within the city and thickly populated districts, or
      whenever such vehicle meets another vehicle on any public highway.

Section 15. Right-Side Driving
    Unless a different course of action is required in the interest of the safety and security of life,
person or property, or because of unreasonable difficulty of operation in compliance herewith,
every person operating a motor vehicle or an animal-drawn vehicle on a highway shall pass to
the right when meeting persons or vehicles coming toward him, and to left when overtaking
persons or vehicles going the same direction, and when turning to the left in going from the
highway to another, every vehicle shall be conducted to the right of the center of the intersection
of the highway.

Section 16. Safety and Awareness
   a) To ensure safety of any pedestrian and awareness of the drivers, it is mandatory that
      there shall be installed speed limit signage in coordination with the Municipal Traffic Aid
      (MUTRA) at the restricted areas specifically school zones, hospitals, markets, churches
      and in the blind curve roads in the Municipality of Loon.

   b) There shall be regulation on the minimum and maximum seating capacity of any
      vehicles specifically public transportations passing within the municipality.

      prudent speed, not greater nor less than is reasonable and proper, having due regard for
      the traffic, the width of the highway, and of any condition then and there existing.
   b) No person shall drive any motor vehicle upon a highway at such a speed as to endanger
      the life, limb and property of any person.

Section 18. Operating Conditions

   a) The maximum allowable speed limit of any vehicle should strictly be followed by drivers.
   b) Over speeding is prohibited but not applicable for the following exemptions as stated in
      Section 17 d.

   c) Driver must slow down and follow the speed limit signages when passing through the
      school zones, municipal hall, churches, hospitals, markets and in the blind curve roads.

   d) Overloaded vehicles are prohibited to transport passengers. Its seating capacity should
      strictly be followed.

   e) No person shall drive a motor vehicle while under the influence of liquor or narcotic drug.

Section 19. Use of Plate Numbers

   a) No vehicle shall be allowed to pass to any street of this locality without bearing a plate
      number, except new ones pending process at the LTO, or non-availability of the plate

   b) No vehicle shall improperly place the plate number, except at the back of the motor

   c) No vehicle shall make or use the “fake plate number“

   d) No vehicle shall use the plate number of another vehicle.

Section 20. Overtaking a Vehicle

     The driver of any motor vehicle overtaking another vehicle proceeding in the same direction
shall pass at a safe distance to the left thereof and shall not again drive to the right side of the
highway until safety clear of such overtaken vehicle, except that, on the highway, within a
business or residential district, having two or more lanes for the movement of traffic in one
direction, the driver of a vehicle may overtake and pass another vehicle on the right. Nothing in
this Article shall be construed to prohibit a driver overtaking and passing upon the right of other
vehicle which is taking or about to make a left turn.

Section 21. Giving Way of Overtaking Vehicle

    The driver of a vehicle about to be overtaken by another vehicle approaching from the rear
shall give way to the overtaking vehicle on suitable and audible signal being given by the driver
of the overtaking vehicle, and shall not increase the speed of his vehicle until completely passed
by the overtaking vehicle.

Section 22. Restrictions on Overtaking and Passing

   a) The driver of a vehicle shall not drive to the left side of the center line of a highway in
      overtaking or passing another vehicle proceeding in the same direction, unless such left
      side is clearly visible, and is free of on coming traffic for a sufficient distance ahead to
      permit such overtaking or passing to be made in safety.

   b) The driver of a vehicle shall not overtake or pass another vehicle proceeding in the same
      direction, when approaching the crest of a grade, nor upon a curve in the highway,
      where the driver’s view along the highway is obstructed within a distance of five hundred
      (500) feet ahead, except on a highway having two or more lanes for movement of traffic
      in one direction where the driver of a vehicle may overtake or pass another vehicle;
      Provided, that on a highway, within a business of residential district, having two or more
      lanes for movement of traffic in one direction, the driver of a vehicle may overtake or
      pass another vehicle on the right.

   c) The driver of a vehicle shall not overtake or pass any other vehicle proceeding in the
      same direction, at any railway grade crossing, nor at any intersection of highways,
      unless such intersection or crossing is controlled by a watchman or a peace officer,
      except on a highway two or more lanes for movement of traffic in one direction where
      the driver of a vehicle may overtake or pass another vehicle on the right. Nothing in this
      section shall be construed to prohibit a driver overtaking or passing, upon the right,
      another vehicle which is making or about to make a left turn.

   d) The driver of a vehicle shall not overtake or pass, or attempt to overtake or pass, any

Section 23. Right of Way

   The following rules shall be observed in yielding the right of way:

   a) When two vehicles approach or enter an intersection at the same time, the driver of the
      vehicle on the left shall yield the right of way to the vehicle on the right, except as
      otherwise provided herein. The driver of any vehicle traveling at any unlawful speed
      forfeit any right of way which he might otherwise have hereunder.

   b) The driver of a vehicle approaching but not having entered an intersection, shall yield the
      right of way to a vehicle within such intersection of turning therein to the left across the
      line of travel of such first mentioned vehicle, provided the driver of the vehicle turning left
      has given a plainly visible signal of intention to turn as required therein.

   c) The driver of any vehicle upon a highway within a business or residential district shall
      yield the right of way to a pedestrian crossing such highway within a crosswalk, except
      an intersection where the movement of traffic is being regulated by a peace officer or by
      traffic signal. Every pedestrian crossing a highway within a business or residential
      district, at any point other than crosswalk, shall yield the right of way to vehicles upon the
      highway.

   d) The driver of a vehicle upon a highway shall bring full stop such vehicle before traversing
      any “through street” or railroad crossing; Provided, hazard exists, the vehicle may be
      slowed down to ten (10) kilometers per hour instead of bringing it to a full stop.
Section 24. Exemption to Right of Way Rule

   a) The driver of a vehicle entering a highway from a private road or drive shall yield the
      right of way to all vehicles approaching on such highway.

   b) The driver of a vehicle upon a highway shall yield the right of way to a police or fire
      department vehicle or ambulance when such vehicles is being operated on official
      business and the driver thereof sounds audible signal of his approach.

   c) The driver of a vehicle entering a “through street” or “stop intersection” shall yield the
      right of way to all vehicles approaching in either direction on such “through street”.
      Provided, that nothing in this Section shall be construed as relieving the driver of any
      vehicle being operated on a “through street or highway” from the duty of driving with due
      regard to the safety of vehicles entering such highway or street nor as protecting the
      said driver from the consequence of an arbitrary exercise of such right of way.

Section 25. Signal on Starting, Stopping or Turning

   The following rules shall be observed before starting, stopping or turning a vehicle:

 a) The driver of any vehicle upon a highway, before starting, stopping or turning from a direct

Section 26. Turning at Intersections

   The following shall be observed when turning at intersections:

   a) The driver of a vehicle intending to run to the right at an intersection shall approach such
       intersection in the lane for traffic nearest to the right-hand side of the highway and, in
       turning, shall keep as close as possible to the right-hand curb or edge of the highway.

   b) The driver of a vehicle intending to turn to the left shall approach such intersection in the
       lane for the traffic to the right of and nearest to the center of line of the highway, and in
       turning, shall pass to the left of the center of the intersection, except that, upon highways
       lane for traffic and upon one-way streets, a left turn shall be made from the left lane of
       traffic in the direction in which the vehicle is proceeding.

   c) For the purpose of this Section, the center of the intersection shall mean the meeting of
       the medial lines of the highways intersecting one another, except when it is occupied by
       a monument, grass plot or any permanent structure, other than a traffic device.

Section 27. Illegal Stopping, Standing or Parking in Specified Places

   No person shall stop, stand or park a vehicle or motorcycle except when necessary to avoid
conflict with other traffic or in compliance with law or directions of a police officer or traffic
control device in any of the following places:

         a)   On a sidewalk, except as otherwise provided herein;
         b)   Within four feet of either of a public or private driveway;
         c)   Within an intersection;
         d)   Within six meters of the intersection of curb lines;

         e)   Within four meters of the driveway entrance to any fire station;
         f)   Within four meters of a fire hydrant;
         g)   On a crosswalk; and
         h)   Within thirty meters upon the approach of any control device.

Section 42. Overloading of Passengers

   A) Overloading shall mean to load passengers over its seating capacity.
   B) No operator or driver /or conductor of passenger vehicles shall load their respective
      public utility vehicles with passengers more than what has been authorized or more than
      the registered capacity of such passenger vehicles or trucks operating within the
      territorial jurisdiction of this municipality. (Sec. 1, MO 1960-20a)
   C) No tricycle or motorcycle for hire shall load passengers in excess of its loading/sitting
      capacity.
   D) Seating Capacity. The seating capacity of a public utility vehicle shall be as follows:

       1) Tricycles
        a) Closed/ Cargo Types – 3 passengers (2 front seat, 1 back ride)
        b) Open-back/ Dual Type – 6 passengers (2 front seat, 2 back seat, 2 behind the driver)
        c) Jeepney Type – 8 passengers (2 front seat, 4 back seat, 2 behind the driver)

       2) Jeepneys – the number of passengers is equal to the allocated seating capacity of the
       vehicle, including seat extensions, as approved by the Land Transportation Office,
       disallowing any hitching or top loads.

       3) Buses - the number of passengers is equal to the allocated seating capacity of the
       vehicle. Any passenger on standing position is considered overloading, and therefore,
       punishable. Any hitching and top loads is prohibited and punishable.

       4) Multicab (PUV) – number of passengers is equal to the allocated seating capacity of
       the vehicle with no seat extensions and strictly no hitching and top loads.

       5) V-hire – number of passengers is equal to the allocated seating capacity of the
       vehicle and front seat must be occupied by only one person so that seat belt can
       properly be used.

       6) Automobiles/ taxi – the number of passengers is equal to the allocated seating
       capacity depending on the types of the automobile and front seat must be occupied by
       only one person so that seat belt can properly be used.

            Any excess of passengers in violation to what is allowed is considered overloading,
       hence, punishable.

 REGULATION OF THE EXISTING OPERATION OF MOTORCYCLE-FOR-LEASE/HIRE OR

Section 64. Rationale

   a) Under Section 7 of Republic Act 4136, or the Land Transportation and Traffic Code,
      which allows the classification of the following:
   • Private passenger automobiles
   • Private trucks
   • Private motorcycle, scooter, or motor wheel attachments

    “Motor vehicles registered under these classifications shall not be used for hire under any
circumstances and shall not be used to solicit, accept or be used to transport passengers or
freight for pay,” per LTFRB mandate.

   b) Under General Welfare Clause of the Local Government Code – Sections 5(c) & 16,
      Chapter 2, Title One, Book I of RA 7160
         • Section 5. Rules of Interpretation – In the interpretation of the provisions of this
            Code, the following rules shall apply:
                 (C) The general welfare provisions in this Code shall be liberally interpreted
                 to give more powers to local government units in accelerating economic
                 development and upgrading the quality of life for the people in the
                 community;
         • Section 16. General Welfare – every local government unit shall exercise the
            powers expressly granted. Those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as
            powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective
            governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general
            welfare. Within their respective territorial jurisdictions, local government units
            shall ensure and support, among other things, the preservation and enrichment
            of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a
            balanced ecology, encourage and support the development of appropriate and
            self-reliant scientific and technological capabilities, improve public morals,
            enhance economic prosperity and social justice, promote full employment among


                                                                                            27


Page 28 of 46
SB MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 18-005, Series of 2018

               their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and
               convenience of their inhabitants.

   c) Police Power – Police power is inherent in the State but not in municipal corporations.
      For a municipal corporation to exercise police power, there must be a legislative grant
      which necessarily also sets the limits for the exercise of the power. In the Philippines,
      the grant of authority is embodied in Sec. 2238 of the Revised Administrative Code,
      otherwise known as the general welfare clause. Chartered cities are granted similar
      authority in their respective charters.

              The local government unit is empowered to exercise police power under the
       LGC, thus: Every local government unit shall:
         • Exercise the power expressly granted.
         • Those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate,
              or incidental for its efficient and effective governance.
         • Those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare. (Sec. 16,
              LGC)

   d) Explanatory Note – Explanatory note of Article IV, Art II, Phil. Constitution, state that the
      enactment of this Traffic and Transportation Code is pursuant to the Constitutional
      Principles that the prime duty of the government is to serve and protect its people and to
      the maintenance of peace and order, to the protection of life, liberty, and property, and
      promotion of the general welfare are essential for the enjoyment by all the people of the
      blessings of democracy.

   e) There is an existing motorcycle for hire locally known as “habal-habal” in this
      municipality since year 2007 more or less and illegally operating and transporting
      passengers without regulations of this LGU; hence, the need for public safety measures.

Section 66. Definition. The following shall be defined and mean as follows:

   a) Motorcycle-for-lease or habal-habal – a motorcycle which offers transport service upon
      demand without a fix rate or shall be pre-determined by mutual agreement between
      passenger and driver.

   b) Regulatory Receipt – is a document issued to the owner of the motorcycle-for-lease
      evidencing full payment of the fees imposed and authority given to operate.

   c) Terminal Area – an area designated for motorcycle-for-lease or habal-habal for loading
      and unloading of passengers for public convenience and safety.

   d) Municipal Sticker – a sticker which shall contain the seal or name of the municipality of
      Loon and a bold number, for easy supervision and inspection, which shall be placed in
      front of the motorcycle and visible to the eyes of traffic enforcers (MUTRA) or
      apprehending officers of this LGU.

   e) Duly registered motorcycle – only motorcycles possessing complete and valid
      registration documents and other documents issued by the Land Transportation Office
      (LTO) shall be allowed to operate as motorcycle-for-lease/hire within the municipality.

                  sidewalks, ramps, railings and the like.

              b) In the case of parking place of any of the above institutions, buildings or establishments,

                                                ARTICLE XII
                   ISSUANCE OF CITATION TICKETS, ARREST AND CLEARANCE

   Section 85. Rules in the Issuance of Citation Tickets. The following are the rules in the
   issuance of Citation Tickets:

         a) Any person who violates any of the provisions of this Code, rules and regulations,

            its specific penal provision of this Code, or in its general sense as covered in the same
            Article

         b) Traffic enforcers to include the MUTRA, PNP, Auxiliary Police and those authorized by
            the Mayor to assist in the enforcement of the traffic laws, rules and regulations,
            ordinances and Executive Order.

         c) Proceeds of the fine shall go to the coffer of the Municipal Treasurer’s office.

         d) The violator who was issued a Citation Ticket is given 7 days or 168 hours within which
            to settle or pay his “administrative fine”, failure of which shall cause for the filing of an
            appropriate case in court.

Section 87. Sharing Scheme of Fines


   The administrative fines imposed in violation of the Traffic Rules and Regulations and the
provisions as embodied in the Traffic Code/ Ordinances shall be shared as follows:

   a) 75% - LGU Loon General Fund
   b) 25% - shall be allocated for the operational expenses of MTMO and MUTRA personnel

Section 88. Procedure upon Apprehension

   a) In every case when a citation is issued the original of the same shall be given to the
      violator or in the case of an unattended vehicle the original of the same shall be affixed
      to said vehicle.

   b) Any vehicles involved in any accidents or violations such as unregistered vehicles, no
      drivers license, driving minors, drunk driving, illegal muffler, no Mayor’s Permit, no LTO
      for hire or MTOP franchise, stolen vehicles, and other illegal acts shall be deposited at
      the designated impounding area of the LGU.

Section 89. Clearance

   a) No renewal of the Driver’s license or registration of motor vehicle or motorcycle shall be
      allowed by the Land Transportation unless a clearance is first secured from the
      Municipal Police Traffic Law Enforcement Division. The purpose of the clearance is to
      show whether the driver or owner of a vehicle or motorcycle has derogatory record with
      the Municipal Traffic Division and to prevent the violator from deliberately evading
      payment of delinquent fines.

   b) No franchise shall be approved or renewed without a MUTRA Clearance certifying that
      the tricycle operator or driver has no liability or has settled its fines and other traffic
      violations.

                                              ACCIDENTS

Section 90. Duty of Driver in Case of Accident

        In the event that any accident should occur as a result of the operation of the motor
vehicle upon a highway, the driver of that vehicle shall stop immediately, and, if required by any
person present shall show his driver’s license, give his true name and address and also the true
name and address of the owner of the motor vehicle.

       No driver of the motor vehicle concerned in a vehicular accident shall leave the scene of
the accident without aiding the victim, except under any of the following circumstances:

   a) If he is in imminent danger of being seriously harmed by any person or persons by
      reason of the accident;

   b) If he reports the accidents to the nearest officer of the law; or

   c) If he has to summon the physician or nurse to aid the victim.

Section 91. Accidents Involving Death or Personal Injuries

        The driver of any vehicle in an accident resulting in injury or death of any person shall
immediately stop such vehicle at the scene of accident or as close thereto as possible but shall
then forthwith return to and in every event shall remain at the scene of the accident until he has
fulfilled the requirements provided herein. Every such stop shall be made without obstructing
traffic any more than is necessary.

Section 92. Accident Involving Damages to a Vehicle

     The driver of any vehicle involved in an accident resulting only in damage to a vehicle
which is driven or attended by any person shall immediately stop such vehicle at the scene of
the accident or as close thereto as possible but shall forthwith return to and in every event shall
remain at the scene of such accident until he has fulfilled the requirements of Section 3, Article
V. Every such stop shall be made without obstructing traffic any more than is necessary.

Section 93. When Drivers Unable to Report

     Whenever the driver of the vehicle is physically incapable of giving an immediate notice of
the accident as herein required and there was another occupant in the vehicle at the time of the
accident capable of doing so, such occupant shall make or cause to be given the notice not
given by the driver.

Section 94. Police Department to Tabulate and Analyze

   The police department shall tabulate and may analyze all traffic accident reports and shall
publish annually or at more frequent intervals statistical information based thereon as to the
number and circumstances or traffic accident.
