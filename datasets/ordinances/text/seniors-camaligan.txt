                               ORDINANCE NO. 004-008
                                    Series 2008
          AN ORDINANCE REGULATING OR SETTING GUIDELINES FOR
          AVAILING THE MEDICAL AND DEATH ASSISTANCE OF
          SENIOR CITIZENS WHO ARE MEMBERS OF THE FEDERATION
          OF SENIOR CITIZEN, CAMALIGAN CHAPTER, CAMALIGAN,
          CAMARINES SUR.
      WHEREAS, section 17 of RA 7160 mandates all local government units to
enhance the delivery of social welfare services to all sectors including the senior citizens;
        WHEREAS, the local government unit recognizes the call to adopt measures for
the protection of well-being of the elderly and extend necessary assistance for a decent
burial service in case of death;
       WHEREAS, while RA 9257, otherwise known as the "Expanded Senior Citizens
Act of 2003", has given due privilege and protection for this sector, a local counterpart
needs be considered in furtherance of this Act;
         WHEREAS, the LGU upholds the customary Filipino tradition of giving utmost
care for the aged, affirming their importance in the community and providing support
until they passed away.
       NOW, THEREFORE, on motion by Councilor Marissa F. Trinidad, unanimously
seconded by all the members present,
        BE IT ORDAINED by the Sangguniang Bayan of Camaligan, Camarines Sur
that:
      ARTICLE I. TITLE AND SCOPE – This ordinance shall be known as the
Medical and Death Assistance for Senior Citizens of Camaligan, Camarines Sur.
       This ordinance shall regulate or set the guidelines on how to avail medical and
death assistance for Senior Citizens.
       ARTICLE II. DEFINITION OF TERMS – As used in this ordinance, the
following shall be construed as:-
a) SENIOR CITIZEN – Any person who is 60 years old and above and a member of
   the Federation of Senior Citizen, Camaligan Chapter, Camaligan, Camarines Sur;

b) FEDERATION – A municipal-wide consortium of senior citizen’s organization with
   complete set of officers and members, and accredited by the Sangguniang Bayan of
   Camaligan, Camarines Sur;
c) ASSISTANCE – Any amount, project, and whatsoever benefit availed by a senior
   citizen;
d) FAMILY – shall be the senior citizen’s legal spouse, his/her children, his/her parents,
   brothers and sisters, and close relative in this order of priority;
        ARTICLE III. IMPOSITION – The following requirements shall be complied by
all senior citizens or their families who shall avail the medical or death assistance:-
1) MEDICAL ASSISTANCE
     a) Certificate of Indigency from the Barangay Captain;
     b) Certificate of Eligibility from DSWD office;
     c) Senior Citizen ID;
     d) Prescription from the Doctor.
       A senior citizen who is hospitalized / confined in the hospital shall receive the
amount of one thousand pesos (Php 1,000.00) once in a year, as medical assistance.
        An ailing senior citizen who is not confined in the hospital or is considered out-
patient may avail of five hundred pesos (Php 500.00) medical assistance once in a year.
2) DEATH ASSISTANCE
     a) Senior Citizen ID;
     b) Death Certificate from the Local Civil Registrar;
     c) Certification from the BASCA President, being a member of Senior Citizen.
       The family of the deceased senior citizen may avail of Two Thousand Pesos (Php
2,000.00) as death benefit.
       ARTICLE IV. REPEALING CLAUSE – All laws, rules and regulations,
ordinances that are inconsistent with the provisions thereof are hereby repealed or
amended accordingly.
         ARTICLE V. PENAL PROVISION – Any person, public or private, found guilty
of falsifying documents to avail of the benefits or to provide the benefits stipulated hereto
shall be penalized as follows:
a) A fine of Php 500.00 for the first offense.
b) A fine of Php 1,000.00 for succeeding offense.
        ARTICLE VI. APPROPRIATIONS - For purposes of this ordinance, the amount
of One Hundred Thousand Pesos (Php 100,000.00) shall be appropriated and provided for
in the annual budget of the Local Government Unit of Camaligan, Camarines Sur.
        Release of assistance shall be limited only until the appropriated amount
stipulated is exhausted unless otherwise the executive branch has certified for the
existence of funds and the legislative branch has taken necessary action for re-
appropriations thereof.
        ARTICLE VII. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE – If, for any reasons, any section or
provision of this ordinance shall be held unconstitutional or invalid by any court or any
competent jurisdiction, such judgment shall not affect or impair the other provisions or
part thereof which shall continue to be enforceable and in effective.
