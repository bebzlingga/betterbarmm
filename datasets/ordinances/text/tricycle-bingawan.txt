MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 238
(Series of 2017)

AN ORDINANCE PRESCRIBING RULES AND REGULATIONS IN THE ISSUANCE OF FRANCHISE TO
TRICYCLES FOR HIRE IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BINGAWAN, PROVINCE OF ILOILO.

Principal Sponsor: SB Member Leovy C. Simora
Co-Sponsor: Committee on Transportation and Communications and the Committee on Legal
Matters, Privileges, Ordinances & Legal Matters

BE IT ORDAINED by the Sangguniang Bayan of the Municipality of Bingawan, Province of Iloilo
that:

SECTION 1. DEFINITION OF TERMS. A tricycle-for-hire is a motor vehicle composed of
motorcycle with a single wheel sidecar or motorcycle with two wheel rear cab. The former having a

total of three wheels, which is operated to tender transport services to the general public for a fee.

Motorized Tricycle Operator’s Permit or MTOP is the franchise document or license to operate

issued to a person, natural or judicial, allowing him to operate a tricycle service over areas or zones
specified therein.

A zone is a contiguous land area or block, subdivision or barangay where a tricycle may operate
without a fixed origin and destination.

c. A common color shall be imposed on tricycle operating in the same zone. Each unit
shall bear a unique identification number aside from its Land Transportation Office {LTOQ)
registration plate;

d. An operator wishing to stop services completely, or to suspend service for more than
one month, shall report such termination to the municipality. No operator shall be prevented

from withdrawing from business of tricycles-for-hire. However, transfer into another zone may

be permitted upon application.

e. The term of the MTOP is one (1) year, subject to renewal. It may be suspended or
cancelled upon due hearing, by the Sangguniang Bayan. Transfer or ownership or zone shall be
constued as an amendment to an MTOP and shall require appropriate approval of the

h. In general, tricycle shall be allowed to operate like a taxi service, rendered upon its
service demand and without a fixed route within a zone.

SECTION 8. FRANCHISE FESS AND CHARGES. There shall be franchise and other
charges/fees to be imposed to tricycle operators annually except of the time to be imposed only to

a) Franchising Fee P 500.00
b) Filing Fee per Unit (one-time payment) 500.00
c) Privileges Fee (new applicants) 200.00
d} Tricycle Inspection Fee 100.00

SECTION 9. PENAL PROVISIONS. Owners and operators of tricycles-for-hire operating within

the municipality without a valid MTOP shall be fined the amount of One Thousand Pesos (P1,000.00)
or imprisonment of three (3) months or both at the discretion of the court.
