AN ORDINANCE CREATING THE PROVINCIAL DISASTER RISK
REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT OFFICE (PDRRMO) IN THE PROVINCE
OF BATAAN.

WHEREAS, on 27 May 2010, Republic Act No. 10121 (R.A.
10121), otherwise known as the Philippine Disaster Risk Reduction and
Management (PDRRM) Act of 2010, was enacted and approved by the
President;

WHEREAS, Section 1, Rule 6 of the Implementing Rules of RA
10121 mandates the enactment of an ordinance for the establishment
of Provincial Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office
(PDRRMO) under the Office of the Governor;

NOW THEREFORE, on motion by Board Member Gerardo R.

RESOLVED, as it is hereby resolved, that the Sangguniang
Panlalawigan of Bataan does hereby ordain:

SECTION 1. Establishment and Functions of Provincial Disaster
Risk Reduction and Management Office of Bataan. - There is hereby
established an Office to be known as the Provincial Disaster Risk
Reduction and Management Office (PDRRMO). The PDRRMO, in
cooperation and coordination with the national government and other
neighboring provinces, shall be tasked to discharge the following
functions:

a. Design, program, and coordinate disaster risk reduction
and management activities consistent with the National
Risk Reduction and Management Council’s standards and
guidelines;

b. Facilitate and support risk assessments and contingency
planning activities at the provincial level;

cs Consolidate local disaster risk information which includes
natural hazards, vulnerabilities, and climate change risks,
and maintain a local risk map;

d. Conduct seminars, training and drills on disaster risk
reduction and management at provincial level;

e. To run and operate early warning system in connection to ;
disaster risk reduction to provide information to national
government agencies and other neighboring provinces and
to the constituents via radio, telecommunications and ‘
social network system

&

f. Formulate and implement a comprehensive and integrated
Provincial Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Plan
(PDRRMP) in accordance with the national, regional and
provincial framework, and policies on disaster risk
reduction in close coordination with the Local Development
Council (LDC);

Prepare and submit to the Sangguniang Panlalawigan
through the Provincial Disaster Risk Reduction and
Management Council (PDRRMC) and the LDC the annual
PDRRMO Plan and Budget, the proposed programming of
the Local Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Fund
(LDRRMF), other dedicated disaster risk reduction and
management resources, and other regular funding
source/s and budgetary support of the LDRR or
BDRRMC;

SECTION 2. COMPOSITION OF THE PDRRMO. - The functions
and responsibilities of the PDRRMO shall be discharged by a Provincial
Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Officer assisted by three (3)

| ~ staffs who will be responsible for administration and training, research
/ \ and planning, and operations and warning, respectively:

a. The Provincial Disaster Risk Reduction and Management
Officer shall possess a Bachelor of Science degree relevant
to the job, with three (3) years of relevant experience on

disaster risk reduction and management;

b. The staff for administration and training shall possess a
bachelor degree relevant to the job with two (2) years of
relevant experience on office management and training;

SECTION 3. FUNDS OF THE PDRRMO. - The budget necessary

for the operation of the PDRRMO to be taken from the Local Disaster

Risk Reduction and Management Fund (LDRRMF) shall be endorsed by

the Provincial Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council to the

Ss Sangguniang Panlalawigan for the enactment of the corresponding
appropriation ordinance;

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. - If for any reason, any
part or provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or
invalid, other parts or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby
shall continue to be in full force and effect;

SECTION 5. REPEALING CLAUSE. All Local Ordinances, Acts,
Executive Orders, Decrees, Proclamations and Administrative
Regulations or part or parts thereof which are inconsistent with any of
the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified
accordingly;
