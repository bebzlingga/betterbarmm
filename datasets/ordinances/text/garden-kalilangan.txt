MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE NO. 2026- 546 (12™ SB)
(29™ Regular Session)

AN ORDINANCE’  INSTITUTIONALIZING THE MUNICIPAL
GARDENING PROGRAM OF KALILANGAN TO PROMOTE FOOD
SECURITY AND ADDRESS MALNUTRITION

AUTHORED BY: HON. JHYA P. AURELIO-TULIC
Chair, Committee on Health & Sanitation and Nutrition

EXPLANATORY NOTE:

WHEREAS, Section 16 of the Local Government Code of 1991
(Republic Act No. 7160) empowers local government units to exercise
powers essential to the promotion of the general welfare of their
constituents;

WHEREAS, Section 17 of the same Code mandates LGUs to deliver
basic services and facilities, including agricultural support services that
promote productivity and self-reliance;

WHEREAS, The Department of the Interior and Local Government
(DILG), through Memorandum Circular No. 2023-001, launched the HAPAG
sa Barangay Program to institutionalize the establishment of barangay
community gardens nationwide;

WHEREAS, The Department of Education (DepEd) implements the Gulayan sa
Paaralan Program to promote school-based food production and nutrition education;

WHEREAS, The Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps) of the Department of
Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) supports initiatives that enhance households food
security and livelihood sustainability, and recognizes backyard and community gardening
as complementary activities that promote nutrition, resilience, and responsible parenting
among beneficiary households;

WHEREAS, The Municipality of Kalilangan recognizes the need to strengthen and
expand these initiatives by institutionalizing backyard gardening at the household level,
barangay community gardens, and school gardens;

WHEREAS, Partnerships with Civil Society Organizations (CSOs), Non-Government
Organizations (NGOs), and local institutions are essential for technical assistance, capacity
building, and resource mobilization;

WHEREAS, This Ordinance aligns with national programs and ensures compliance with
constitutional principles while promoting sustainable food security and community
resilience.

WHEREFORE, viewed from the light of the foregoing premises and upon the motion
made by HON. JHYA P. AURELIO-TULIC, jointly seconded by HON. WILDRED P.
RAMAS and HON. LOIDO L. PADSING, the AUGUST BODY HAS:

RESOLVED, enacting the following Municipal Ordinance:

MUNICIPAL GENERAL ORDINANCE NO. 2026-546 (12'" SB)
(29™ Regular Session)

Be it enacted by the Sanggunniang Bayan of Kalilangan, Bukidnon Province of Bukidnon in
session assembled THAT:

SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as the “Kalilangan Municipal
Gardening Program Ordinance.”

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. It is the declared policy of the Municipality
of Kalilangan to institutionalize a comprehensive and inclusive gardening program that
ensures the active participation of households, barangays, schools, and partner
organizations in sustainable gardening initiatives as a strategic approach to achieving food
security, improving nutrition, and addressing malnutrition.

The Local Government Unit (LGU) shall prioritize vulnerable and
marginalized sectors, including nutritionally at-risk families, Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino
Program (4Ps) beneficiary households, pregnant and lactating mothers, and children, to
guarantee equitable access to fresh, safe, and nutritious food.

Furthermore, the Municipality shall promote community empowerment
and self-reliance by encouraging collective action, strengthening coordination with national
government agencies—particularly the Department of Social Welfare and Development
(DSWD) and the Department of Agriculture (DA)—fostering partnerships with Civil Society
Organizations (CSOs), Non-Government Organizations (NGOs), and the private sector, and
supporting innovations in organic farming, climate-resilient, and resource-efficient
gardening technologies.

SECTION 3. OBJECTIVES. The objectives of this ordinance are as follows:

1. Promote a progressive increase in household participation in backyard gardening
across all barangays, with particular focus on low-income and food-insecure
households.

2. Institutionalize barangay-level community gardens with designated coordinators,
clear maintenance and sustainability plans, and active participation of residents,
including Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps) beneficiary households.

3. Strengthen and sustain school gardens under the Department of Education’s
Gulayan sa Paaralan Program to enhance nutrition education, food production,
and learner engagement.

4. Prioritize vulnerable and nutritionally at-risk sectors, pregnant and lactating
mothers, children, and other food-insecure families, in accessing gardening
resources, capacity-building activities, and technical support.

5. Mobilize Civil Society Organizations (CSOs), Non-Government Organizations
(NGOs), private entities, and local institutions as partners, and encourage their
active participation through the establishment of their own gardens and the
provision of support—such as seeds, tools, technical assistance, and knowledge
sharing—to community and school gardens.

6. Integrate livelihood and food security opportunities by promoting value addition,
responsible surplus produce marketing, and the adoption of climate-resilient and
resource-efficient gardening practices to enhance household income and
community resilience.

SECTION 4. DEFINITION OF TERMS. Terms used in this ordinance shall mean:

1. Backyard Gardening - The cultivation of vegetables, fruits, herbs, and other
food crops within household premises for family consumption, nutritional
supplementation, and possible income generation.

2. Community Garden - A shared gardening space managed by the barangay,
intended for food production, skills development, and community engagement,
particularly benefiting households without adequate land for cultivation.

3. School Garden - A designated gardening space within school premises utilized
for food production, nutrition education, and practical learning activities,
supervised by school heads in coordination with teachers and relevant school
personnel.

4. Organic Gardening — A method of gardening that avoids the use of synthetic
fertilizers and pesticides, relying instead on composting, natural inputs, and
environmentally sound farming practices.

5. Garden Coordinator - A _ barangay-designated individual responsible for
overseeing the planning, establishment, maintenance, reporting, and community
mobilization related to barangay community gardens.

6. Capacity-Building - Activities such as training, workshops, and educational
sessions aimed at enhancing knowledge, skills, and attitudes related to gardening,
nutrition, and sustainable food production.

7, Climate-Resilient Practices — Gardening techniques and approaches designed
to adapt to climate variability and extreme weather conditions, including
water-efficient systems, diversified cropping, and soil conservation measures.

8. 4Ps Gardening - The participation of Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps)
beneficiary households in backyard, community, or other approved gardening
activities supported by the Local Government Unit and partner agencies, as a
complementary intervention to promote household food security, nutrition, and
self-reliance, without imposing additiona! conditionalities beyond existing program
requirements.

SECTION 5. PROGRAM COVERAGE. This Program shall comprehensively cover all
fourteen (14) barangays, public schools, households, and partner organizations such as Civil
Society Organizations (CSOs), Non-Government Organizations (NGOs), and private entities
within the Municipality of Kalilangan. Implementation shall prioritize areas and households
identified through nutrition and poverty mapping as having high incidence of malnutrition
and food insecurity.

The Program shall cover the following components:

Household / Backyard Gardens - All households in the Municipality shall be
encouraged and assisted to establish and maintain household or backyard food
gardens for daily consumption and possible surplus production for income generation.
e Barangay Community Gardens - Every barangay shall establish and maintain a
designated community garden of not less than two hundred (200) square meters, in
coordination with the Provincial / Municipal Agriculture Office (PAGRO/MAO). These
gardens shall serve primarily as demonstration, training, and supplementary
production areas, and shail not substitute the household gardening requirement of
4Ps beneficiary households.
¢ School Gardens - All public schools within the Municipality shall establish and
maintain functional school gardens integrated into the curriculum in accordance with
the Department of Education’s Gulayan sa Paaralan Program.
¢ Partner Organizations (CSOs, NGOs, and Private Entities) - Civil Society
Organizations, Non-Government Organizations, private institutions, and other
partners are encouraged to establish their own gardens and to assist household,
barangay, and school gardens through resource sharing, technical assistance, and
community engagement activities.

SECTION 6. PUBLIC-PRIVATE PARTNERSHIP AND LAND USE
ARRANGEMENTS. To support the establishment and sustainability of barangay community
gardens, the Local Government Unit and barangays may enter into public-private
partnership arrangements with private individuals, organizations, or institutions.

Barangay communal gardens may utilize privately-owned land, subject to the voluntary
consent of the landowner and the execution of a Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) or
Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) that clearly defines the terms and conditions of land
use, responsibilities of the parties, duration of use, and other applicable provisions, in
accordance with existing laws, rules, and regulations.

Such arrangements shall be undertaken solely for community gardening purposes and
shall not result in any transfer of ownership or permanent encumbrance of private property.

SECTION 7. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES. The following offices, institutions,
and stakeholders shall have the respective roles and responsibilities in the implementation
of this Ordinance:

1. Local Government Unit (LGU)

a. Allocate funds and mobilize resources necessary for the effective implementation
of the Program.

b. Issue policies, guidelines, and implementing issuances, and provide overall
supervision and technical support through concerned offices.

c. Ensure inter-agency coordination among the Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO),
Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO), Department of Social
Welfare and Development (DSWD), Department of Education (DepEd), and
barangays.

d. Conduct regular monitoring, evaluation, and assessment of program
~ implementation, with particular focus on nutrition outcomes and compliance with
program objectives.

2. Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO)
a. Serve as the lead implementing and technical agency of the Program.

b. Provide technical assistance, training, and extension services on organic
gardening, composting, pest management, climate-resilient, and space-efficient
gardening techniques.

c. Distribute seeds, seedlings, planting materials, and basic gardening tools,
prioritizing nutritionally at-risk households and Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program
an) (4Ps) beneficiary families.

d. Assist barangays and 4Ps households in developing appropriate backyard or
alternative gardening models suited to available space and local conditions.

3. Barangays

a. Mobilize and encourage households, including Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program
(4Ps) beneficiary households, to establish and maintain household or backyard
gardens.

b. Establish, maintain, and manage barangay community gardens and designate
qualified Garden Coordinators.

c. Monitor the establishment and maintenance of household and community gardens,
including those of 4Ps households, and submit consolidated monthly accomplishment
and progress reports to the Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO).

d. Promote community gardening initiatives and facilitate participation of Civil Society
Organizations (CSOs), Non-Government Organizations (NGOs), and other institutions
within their jurisdiction.

4. Schools

a. Implement and sustain the Department of Education’s Gulayan sa Paaralan

b. Integrate gardening, nutrition, and food security concepts into the school

c. Coordinate with the MAO and submit periodic reports on school garden

7. Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)

a. Provide program coordination and policy guidance in relation to Pantawid

b. Facilitate the integration of gardening, nutrition, and food security topics into

c. Coordinate with the LGU and MAO in monitoring and supporting 4Ps household

conditionalities on cash grants.

8. Civil Society Organizations (CSOs) / Non-Government Organizations
(GNGOs) and Private Partners

a. Actively participate in the Program by establishing and maintaining their own
gardens, where feasible.

b. Colaborate with the LGU, MAO, barangays, and schools in capacity-building,
technical assistance, advocacy, and knowledge-sharing activities.

c. Share resources, expertise, or logistical support to strengthen household,
barangay, and school gardening initiatives.

d. Assist in community mobilization, advocacy, and innovation efforts that promote
sustainable and inclusive food production.

SECTION 8. CAPACITY-BUILDING AND EDUCATION. The Local Government Unit
(LGU), through the Municipal Agriculture Office and Municipal Nutrition Council, shall
conduct quarterly training sessions for households, barangay coordinators, and schools on
organic gardening, composting, pest management, and nutrition education. Community
engagement activities such as garden fairs and nutrition campaigns shall be organized to
promote awareness and participation.

SECTION 9. PROGRAM EVALUATION INDICATORS. The following indicators shall
be used to assess and measure the effectiveness and impact of the Program:

1. Number of gardens established and sustained at the household, barangay, and
school levels, including the percentage of Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps)
beneficiary households with functional household or backyard gardens.

2. Reduction in the prevalence of malnutrition, undernutrition, and related nutrition
risk indicators, particularly among children, pregnant and lactating mothers, and 4Ps
beneficiary households, as reflected in data from the Municipal Nutrition Council,
barangay health stations, and related monitoring systems.

3. Increase in household vegetable consumption and dietary diversity, with specific
attention to improvements among Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps)
beneficiary families, as measured through available nutrition surveys, Food
Consumption Questionnaires, or Family Development Session (FDS) reports.

4. Level of participation and engagement of Civil Society Organizations (CSOs),
Non-Government Organizations (NGOs), private partners, and 4Ps household leaders
in garden establishment, capacity-building activities, advocacy campaigns, and
support initiatives at the barangay and municipal levels.

SECTION 10. INTEGRATION WITH OTHER PROGRAMS. This program shall be
integrated with municipal health, nutrition, and disaster resilience programs to ensure a
holistic approach to food security and community well-being.

SECTION 11. INCENTIVES AND LIVELIHOOD. The Local Government Unit (LGU)
shall establish an annual incentive and recognition system to encourage excellence,
sustainability, and innovation in the implementation of household, barangay, school, and
Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps), Civil Society Organizations (CSOs),
Non-Government Organizations (NGOs) gardening initiatives.

An annual “Best Barangay Garden Award” shail be instituted to recognize barangays
that successfully implement and sustain backyard or household gardens, barangay
community gardens, school gardens, and 4Ps household gardens in accordance with this
Ordinance.

Incentives for top-performing barangays may include a combination of cash
incentives, certificates of recognition, gardening tools, seed and planting material packages,
technical support, and public recognition during municipal or barangay events.

Based on the results of program evaluation and subject to the availability of funds,
the following cash incentives are hereby recommended for top-performing barangays:

e First Place - Twenty Thousand Pesos (P20,000.00)

e Second Place - Fifteen Thousand Pesos (f15,000.00)

e Third Place - Ten Thousand Pesos (P 10,000.00)

e Consolation Prizes - Three Thousand Pesos (P3,000.00) per qualified barangay

e Best Household / Backyard Garden
e Best CSO / NGO Garden

The Municipal Agriculture Office (Municipal DA/MAO), the Municipal Health Office (MHO),

shall include, but shall not be limited to, indicators on garden establishment and
sustainability, household food consumption, nutrition outcomes, and participation of 4Ps
beneficiary households, and shall align with existing national and provincial reporting
systems.

SECTION 12. FUNDING. The implementation of this program shall be financed
through the General Fund of the Local Government Unit and may be augmented by eligible
funding sources such as the Municipal Nutrition Program and Agriculture Program funds,
whenever applicable and permissible under existing laws, guidelines, and auditing
standards.

SECTION 13. MONITORING AND REPORTING RESPONSIBILITIES. The MAO
shall submit quarterly reports to the Sangguniang Bayan detailing program status, number
of gardens established, training sessions conducted, and impact on nutrition indicators.

Barangay officials shall conduct monthly inspections of household gardens and submit
consolidated reports to the Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO). Reports shall include:

e Number of active gardens
e Challenges encountered

e Recommendations for improvement

SECTION 14. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If any provision of this Ordinance is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining provisions shall not be affected and shall
remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 15. REPEALING CLAUSE. All ordinances, rules, and regulations
inconsistent with this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.
