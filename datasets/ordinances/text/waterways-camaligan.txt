                            MUNICIPAL ORDINANCE # 009-008
                                    Series of 2008
          AN ORDINANCE PROHIBITING ANY PERSON OR ENTITY TO
          CLOSE, HINDER, OBSTRUCT, SUSPEND, OR CAUSE THE DELAY OF
          THE FREE FLOW OF RAIN OR FLOOD WATERS IN ANY CANAL,
          CREEK OR ANY EXISTING WATERWAYS WITHIN THE
          JURISDICTION OF CAMALIGAN, CAMARINES SUR BY WHATEVER
          MANNER INCLUDING THE CONSTRUCTION OF ANY STRUCTURE,
          PUTTING ANY MATERIALS THEREIN OR ON ITS BANKS, AND
          PARKING OR DOCKING ANY VEHICLE (LAND AND WATER
          VESSEL) WHETHER THE SAID WATERWAY IS WITHIN OR
          OUTSIDE HIS PROPERTY, AND PROVIDING PENALTIES FOR
          VIOLATIONS THEREOF.
      WHEREAS, this initiative is affected to prevent major flooding, caused by clogged
waterways;
       WHEREAS, health-related concerns has always been one of the top priorities of the LGU
and prevention of outbreak of diseases caused by contaminated, unhygienic stagnant waters
should always be looked into;
       WHEREAS, waterways must always be free from potential pollutants as these are
environmental hazards and may cause disastrous effects in the future;
      NOW THEREFORE, on motion by Councilor Benedicto B. Jacob duly seconded by all
the members present,
       BE IT ORDAINED by the Sangguniang Bayan of Camaligan, Camarines Sur, that:-
       SECTION 1. TITLE AND SCOPE OF ORDINANCE – This ordinance shall be known
as “The Waterways Protection Ordinance of Camaligan, Camarines Sur”.
         It shall cover all creeks, canals, and waterways, whether man-made or natural, within the
territorial jurisdiction of Camaligan, Camarines Sur in order to assure the free flow of rain or
flood waters.
       SECTION 2. DEFINITION OF TERMS – As used in this ordinance:
1) CREEKS – any natural streams or waterways that actually exist and/or are indicated in the
   cadastral map of Camaligan, Camarines Sur;

2) CANALS – any man-made waterways along roadsides or property lines intended for the
   free flow or passage of rain or flood waters which are generally part of the drainage system;
3) WATERWAYS – any man-made or natural channel specifically designed to be a part of
   drainage system.
4) ENTITY – group, association, organization, company, residence, partnership or
   aggrupations with juridical personality;
          SECTION 3. PROHIBITIONS – It is hereby prohibited for any person or entity to:
1) Close, hinder, obstruct, suspend or cause the delay of the free flow of rain or floodwaters in
   any canal, creek, or any existing waterways by whatever manner, whether such is within or
   outside his property;
2) Construct any structure, or to place or dump any material on creeks, canals, or any
   waterways.
3) Park, dock, impede the movement or lay abandon any vehicle, whether land or water vessel
   on any creeks, canals, and waterways, including covered sidewalks;
4) Construct drainage canal or man-made waterways not in accordance with proper elevation
   for the free flow of water.
        SECTION 4. PENALTY – Any person or entity who violates any provision of this
ordinance shall be penalized with a fine of not less than one thousand pesos (Php 1,000.00) and
not more than two thousand five hundred pesos (Php 2,500.00) or an imprisonment of not less
than one (1) month but not more than six (6) months, or both such fine and imprisonment at the
discretion of the court.
       If the violator is an entity, the highest official or the head of the family shall be made
responsible and answerable to suffer the penalty herein mentioned.
        Aside from the penalties, the violators shall remove or demolish all obstructions that were
put up and shall restore the canals, creeks, or any waterways to its original condition upon receipt
of notice of violation, at his expense.
        All entities or individuals who constructed and/or put up any obstructions on the
waterways before this ordinance is enacted shall be given thirty (30) days to remove them.
Failure to comply will be meted with the same penalty as the above.
        SECTION 5. ENFORCEMENT - The Municipal Mayor, the Rural Health Unit, the
PNP, the Barangay Captains and other appropriate government agencies shall effectively enforce
this ordinance.
        SECTION 5. REPEALING CLAUSE – All ordinances, rules, regulations or portions
thereof, inconsistent with this ordinance are hereby repealed, amended, and/or modified
accordingly.
        SECTION 6. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE – if for any reason, any section of this
ordinance is held unconstitutional or invalid, the other provisions hereof remains to continue in
full force and affect.
       SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITY – This ordinance shall take effect immediately upon its
approval.
          ENACTED: July 23, 2008


          WE HEREBY CERTIFY to the correctness and truthfulness of the foregoing.
