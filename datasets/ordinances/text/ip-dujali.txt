CHIEFTAINS, MUNICIPAL IP WOMEN REPRESENTATIVE AND MUNICIPAL IP YOUTH
REPRESENTATIVE NOT HOLDING IPMR POSITIONS IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BRAULIO E. DUJALI,
DEFINING THEIR ROLES AND FUNCTIONS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

EXPLANATORY NOTE

Indigenous Cultural Communities/Indigenous Peoples (ICCs/IPs) in Braulio E. Dujali, uphold a profound
legacy of leadership deeply rooted in customary laws and ancestral traditions. Even in the absence of formal
public office, tribal chieftains at the barangay and municipal levels continue to serve as steadfast pillars of
wisdom, unity, and conflict resolution within their communities. Their roles often carried out without
financial compensation are essential to maintaining peace, preserving cultural integrity, and ensuring
communal harmony.

This ordinance seeks to recognize and remunerate the invaluable service of tribal chieftains and eligible
active leaders from women and youth sectors who do not occupy Indigenous Peoples Mandatory
Representative (IPMR) positions. Their enduring leadership remains crucial in safeguarding indigenous
traditions, fostering cultural continuity, and facilitating collaboration between I[CCs/IPs and government
institutions.

Through the institutionalization of a monthly honorarium program, the Municipal Government reaffirms
its commitment to cultural preservation and inclusive governance. This initiative highlights the
municipality's unwavering support for indigenous leadership systems, which have long served as the
foundation of IP governance, identity, and resilience.

Be it ordained by the Sangguniang Bayan of Braulio E. Dujali, Davao del Norte, in session duly assembled:

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY

The Municipality of Braulio E. Dujali shall institutionalize a monthly honorarium program for duly
recognized Tribal Chieftains in the respective Barangay, Municipal IP Women representative and IP Youth
Leader representative, not holding IPMR positions. This honorarium is not a form of salary, wage, or
employment benefit, but an institutional act of support that seeks to:

Acknowledge the leadership role of IP Leaders within their communities;
Enable them to carry out their responsibilities in conflict mediation, cultural preservation and
community coordination;

Strengthen the cultural integrity of indigenous Peoples within the framework of inclusive local
governance.

SECTION 3. COVERAGE

This ordinance shall apply to Barangay Tribal Chieftains, Municipal IP Women representative and IP Youth
Leader representative in the Municipality of Braulio E. Dujali who are serving as Indigenous Peoples
Mandatory Representatives (IPMRs)

Only one tribal chieftain per barangay and one per municipality shall be eligible to receive the monthly
honorarium under this ordinance.

For purposes of this Ordinance, those holding IPMR positions are excluded from coverage in view of their
entitlements and privileges as public officials, which already include compensation, benefits, and
allowances sourced from government funds. This measure ensures equitable allocation of municipal tribal
leaders who, despite holding no formal government positions, continue to serve their communities in
accordance with indigenous customs and traditions.

SECTION 4. HONORARIA GRANT

Tribal Chieftains, Municipal IP Women representative, IP Youth Leader representative covered by this
Ordinance shall receive monthly honoraria as follows:

A monthly honorarium of One Thousand Pesos (1,000.00) shall be granted to each Barangay Tribal
Chieftain in recognition of their leadership and service to their respective Indigenous communities.

e Amonthly honorarium of One Thousand Pesos (1,000.00) shall likewise be provided to
the Municipal Indigenous Peoples (IP) Women Representative, acknowledging her role in
advocating for the rights and welfare of IP women.

SECTION 5. ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS
To qualify for the grant, the Barangay chieftain must be certified by the National Commission on

Indigenous Peoples (NCIP) as a duly recognized tribal chieftain, upon the recommendation by the
Municipal Tribal Chieftain.

Provided, that Municipal Tribal Chieftains must be certified prior to the certification of Barangay Tribal
Chieftains, to ensure an orderly and legitimate recommendation

SECTION 6. ROLES AND FUNCTIONS

The Municipal IP Leaders/representatives receiving honoraria under this Ordinance are expected to:

Uphold and promote customary laws, traditions, and cultural practices;
Mediate and resolve intra-community conflicts based on indigenous dispute resolution
mechanisms;
Participate in relevant community programs and provincial consultations;
Mural liaisons between their communities and government agencies;

SECTION. IMPLEMENTING OFFICE

The Office of the Municipal Mayor through the Office of the Municipal Administrator shall be responsible
for the implementation of this ordinance, including coordination, monitoring and disbursement of the
monthly honoraria in accordance with applicable laws, rules and regulations.

SECTON 8. FUNDING AND APPROPRIATION

The Municipal Government of Braulio E. Dujali, shall allocate funds annually for the implementation of this
Ordinance, chargeable against its General Fund or any other available funding source, and subject to the
usual budgeting, accounting, and auditing rules and procedures. The actual amount to be disbursed of each
year shall be determined based on the updated list of certified tribal chieftains, recognizing that such
number may vary annually due to changes in IPMR appointments or transitions back to non-IPMR roles.

SECTION 10. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE
If any provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the remaining provisions shall

remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 11. EFFECTIVITY
This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon its approval.
