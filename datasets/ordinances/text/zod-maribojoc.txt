Municipal Ordinance No. 2026-07
Series of 2026

AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING ALL HOUSEHOLDS IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF MARIBOJOC, BOHOL TO
COMPLY WITH THE ZERO OPEN DEFECATION (ZOD) POLICY, PROVIDING GUIDELINES FOR ITS
IMPLEMENTATION, PRESCRIBING PENALTIES FOR VIOLATIONS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS
THEREFOR.

Authored by: Hon. Maria Arlene K. Chiu

WHEREAS, the 1987 Constitution of the Republic of the Philippines declares that the State shall
protect and promote the right to health of the people and instill health consciousness among them;

WHEREAS, Administrative Order No. 2010-0021 of the Department of Health (DOH) declares
Sustainable Sanitation as a National Policy and a National Priority Program to protect public health and the
environment;

WHEREAS, Administrative Order No. 2019-0054 institutionalizes the Philippine Approach to
Sustainable Sanitation (PHATSS) Program nationwide, empowering Local Government Units (LGUs) to
manage sanitation with the goal of achieving Zero Open Defecation in communities and ensuring progress
on sanitation-related Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs);

WHEREAS, the Local Government Unit (LGU) of Maribojoc has been providing, this year, two (2)
latrine toilets in every barangay, and three (3) barangays namely, Barangay Busao, Barangay Toril, and
Barangay Guiwanon, have successfully passed the Zero Open Defecation (ZOD) evaluation.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Sangguniang Bayan of Maribojoc, in session duly
assembled, that:

Section 1. Title. This Ordinance shall be known as the “Zero Open Defecation (ZOD) Compliance Ordinance
of the Municipality of Maribojoc, Bohol.”

Section 2. Declaration of Policy. It is the policy of the Municipality of Maribojoc to promote public health,
sanitation, and environmental protection by ensuring that all households observe the Zero Open Defecation
(ZOD) Policy. The Municipality recognizes that open defecation poses serious health risks, contaminates
water sources, and endangers the well-being of its residents, particularly children.

Section 3. Scope and Coverage. This Ordinance shall apply to all persons, whether residents or non-
residents, within the territorial jurisdiction of the Municipality of Maribojoc, Bohol.

Section 4. Definition of Terms. For the purpose of this Ordinance, the following terms shall mean:

a) Zero Open Defecation (ZOD) - a program aimed at eliminating open defecation, which is the
practice of people defecating in open spaces, by ensuring that every household has access to
sanitary toilets and adheres to proper sanitation and hygiene practices.

b) Defecate — to excrete/discharge fecal waste from the body; to have a bowel movement.

c) Information Education Communication (IEC) Campaign — a strategic effort to raise awareness,
educate the public, and influence behavior through various communication channels, including the
dissemination of information to promote healthy practices and encourage participation in government
programs.

d) Feces — waste matter discharged from the bowels after food has been digested.

€) Open Defecation — the practice of defecating in fields or other open spaces instead of using sanitary
toilets or latrines.

f) Public Space — a place that is open and accessible to the general public and is not exclusively used
for private gatherings or other personal purposes.

g) Sanitary Toilet — a toilet facility that promotes health by safely and properly disposing of human
waste, thereby preventing the spread of disease and environmental contamination.

Section 5. Prohibited Acts.

a) To defecate in open places.

b) To dispose of human waste, including tissue and paper, outside toilets or in any open spaces,
whether public or private.

C) To dispose of human waste in waterways, including rivers, creeks, drains, or canals, in a manner
that contaminates the surface of the water

Section 6. General and Administrative Provisions.

a) Every household within the territorial jurisdiction of the Municipality of Maribojoc shall be required to
have a sanitary toilet in their respective homes to ensure the proper disposal of human waste and to
prevent the occurrence and spread of diseases.

b) The Municipality of Maribojoc shall provide a free toilet bowl, provided that, as a counterpart, the
household owner shall be responsible for digging the septic tank pit, constructing the toilet, and
supplying any additional materials needed for its completion.

c) In the barangays, the Purok Leaders and Barangay Health Workers (BHWs) shall inspect and
monitor households and maintain a master list of all households that do not have sanitary toilets.

d) The Municipal Health Office and the Municipal Sanitary Inspector shall conduct Information,
Education, and Communication (IEC) campaigns in the barangays to prevent the effects of water-
borne diseases, such as typhoid fever, cholera, and hepatitis A, resulting from open defecation and
improper sanitation.

Section 7. Guidelines in Distributing Toilet Bowls. After the Purok Leaders and BHWs compile the list of
households without sanitary toilets, the Municipal Sanitary Inspector shall conduct an initial inspection to

verify that the septic tank pits have been properly dug. A few days after the household has received the toilet
bowl, the Municipal Sanitary Inspector shall conduct a follow-up inspection to ensure that the toilet has been
properly installed and is in place.

Section 8. Enforcement. Upon any proof or evidence of a violation, a complaint shall be presented to the
Barangay Council and forwarded to the Municipal Police Station for further investigation.

Section 9. Penalties. Any person found defecating outside a designated toilet facility shall be penalized as
follows:

a) First Offense - Two Hundred Pesos (P200.00)
b) Second Offense — Five Hundred Pesos (P500.00)
c) Third Offense - One Thousand Pesos (P1,000.00)

Section 10. Barangay Share from Penalty. Fifty percent (50%) of the proceeds from fines or penalties
collected for violations of this Ordinance committed within the territorial jurisdiction of the Barangay shall
accrue to the Barangay, while the remaining fifty percent (50%) shall accrue to the Municipal Government.

Section 11. Guidelines in Claiming the Barangay’s Share from Collected Penalties:

a) Legal Basis Verification - confirm that the municipal ordinance expressly provides 50% barangay
share.

b) Issuance of Citation — a violation is committed within the barangay.

c) Payment of Penalty — the offender shall pay the fine to the Municipal Treasurer's Office. An Official
Receipt (OR) shall be issued, indicating the nature of the violation and the name of the barangay.

d) Recording and Consolidation - the Municipal Treasurer's Office shall record all collected penalties
and prepare a summary report of collections on a semi-annual basis.

e) Computation of Barangay Share

f) Request for Release — a written request, signed by the Punong Barangay, shall be submitted to the
Municipal Treasurer's Office.

g) Review and Approval done by the Treasurer

h) Release/Remittance of Funds

i) Disbursement voucher/acknowledgement receipt is signed by the barangay.

j) Recording by the barangay.

Section 12. Repealing Clause. All ordinances, executive orders, rules, and regulations inconsistent with
this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

Section 13. Separability Clause. If any part or provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or
invalid, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

Section 14. Effectivity. This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.

Enacted by the Sangguniang Bayan of Maribojoc, Bohol on the 27th day of January 2026.
